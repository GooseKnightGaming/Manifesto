*"The law is reason, free from passion."*

## A NOTE ON WHAT FOLLOWS

Some of the provisions below are based on real archaic British and international laws — revived, updated, and applied with appropriate modern context.

Some are genuine policy proposals that happen to be phrased in an entertainingly specific way.

Some are completely, deliberately, magnificently absurd.

All are presented in the spirit of a legal tradition that once made it illegal to die in the Houses of Parliament, required London taxis to carry a bale of hay, and genuinely criminalised the washing of windows with a mop on a Sunday.

Britain has always had a talent for the legally surreal. Britain Forward honours that tradition.

They are divided into four categories:

- **Category One: Reasonable Policy in Entertaining Form**
- **Category Two: Historically Real, Charmingly Revived**
- **Category Three: Civic Traditions — The Five Jiuans**
- **Category Four: Gloriously, Unapologetically Weird**

## CATEGORY ONE: REASONABLE POLICY IN ENTERTAINING FORM

*These have genuine policy merit. The framing is whimsical. The intent is real.*

---

**On Public Noise**

It is an offence to make excessive noise before 10:00 in the morning or after 18:00 in the evening on any weekday or Saturday; and an offence to make excessive noise at any hour on a Sunday.

For the avoidance of doubt, "excessive noise" includes but is not limited to: the operation of power tools without reasonable cause, the conducting of amplified musical performances without a licence, and the vigorous prosecution of laundry in communal housing during the hours specified.

"Excessive" shall be determined by reference to whether a reasonable person in the vicinity would, upon hearing it, use words unprintable in a government document.

*(Note: This reflects existing noise nuisance law, which is already on the statute books and already ignored. Britain Forward commits to enforcing it.)*

---

**On Dogs in Public Spaces**

It is an offence to permit a dog to be off its lead in any public park, urban shopping centre, or enclosed public recreational space, except where explicitly permitted by signage.

Such permissive signage must be approved by the relevant Local Commission and displayed at all principal entrances to the designated area.

A dog that is off its lead in a non-designated area is not the dog's fault. The person attached to the other end of the lead is the responsible party. The law applies accordingly.

---

**On Beach Safety — Hole Depths**

When digging a hole in beach sand, it is an offence for any contiguous excavation to be deeper than six feet, or wider than twelve feet at its widest point.

Two or more holes shall be considered one contiguous excavation if there is less than three feet of undisturbed sand between them.

*(This is a genuine safety provision. People have died in beach holes. Six feet is a grave. Six feet is too deep.)*

---

**On Public Health and Transport**

It is an offence for a person known to be carrying a serious infectious disease — including but not limited to diseases notifiable under current public health legislation — to travel by taxi or scheduled public transport without prior arrangement with the operator and without taking all reasonable precautions against transmission.

Reasonable precautions are defined by current public health guidance at the time of travel.

*(This is existing public health law, codified more plainly.)*

---

**On Alcohol and Licensed Premises**

It is an offence for any licensed premises or its licensee to:

a) Permit drunkenness on the premises
b) Permit quarrelsome or violent behaviour on the premises
c) Sell or supply alcohol to a person who is already intoxicated

*(This has been British law since the Licensing Act 1872. It is rarely enforced. Britain Forward commits to enforcing it.)*

---

**On Chewing Gum and Public Transport**

It is an offence to chew gum whilst on, boarding, alighting from, or waiting for public transport at a designated stop or station.

*(Singapore has banned this since 1992. Singapore's public transport is immaculate. The correlation is noted.)*

---

**On Public Drinking Fountains**

It is an offence to use a public drinking fountain for any purpose other than the consumption of water.

Public drinking fountains are provided for drinking. They are not car-washing facilities. They are not dog-bathing facilities. They are not impromptu paddling pools. They are not art installations. They are drinking fountains. The clue is in the name.

---

**On Persons with Infectious Diseases**

A person carrying a serious notifiable infectious disease must not travel by public transport or taxi unless they have:

a) Informed the operator in advance
b) Taken all reasonable precautions against transmission as defined by current public health guidance
c) Obtained medical clearance where required

This provision does not apply to persons who do not know they are carrying an infectious disease. It applies to persons who do know. The distinction matters.

---

**On Coin-Finding**

Should a person discover coins or notes upon the ground, unattended and unclaimed, the following applies:

A person may take possession of those coins or notes whose obverse (crown side, the side depicting the Monarch or Head of State) is facing upward.

Should all discovered coins or notes have their reverse (the decorative or numerical side) facing upward, a person may take possession of all of them.

A person may not turn unattended coins or notes from one face to the other in order to increase the quantity they may legitimately claim. Such turning shall be treated as an act contrary to the spirit of this provision, and the turner forfeits their claim to all coins and notes at that location.

*(Based on traditional treasure trove principles, updated for the post-gold-standard era.)*

---

**On Armed Persons in the Chambers of Parliament**

No person shall enter the High Chamber or the Low Chamber — nor any recognised anteroom or voting lobby thereof — while bearing arms or wearing armour.

*(This has been British law for centuries. It is listed here because naming it explicitly after the renaming of the chambers feels appropriate. The chambers shall henceforth be known as the High Chamber and the Low Chamber. Names for public consultation.)*

## CATEGORY TWO: HISTORICALLY REAL, CHARMINGLY REVIVED

*These are based on laws that actually existed. Britain Forward brings them into the 21st century.*

---

**On Taxicabs and Their Required Equipment**

Every taxicab operating under a licence granted within Britain must carry, securely stowed in its boot or beneath its front passenger seat, the following items:

a) A dry powder fire extinguisher with a volume of no less than one litre, maintained in working order and within its service date
b) A spare tyre suitable for the taxicab, together with sufficient equipment to effect its replacement
c) Two bottles of drinking water, each of no less than 500ml capacity
d) A bag of hay or straw, of no less than one kilogram in weight

*(The hay requirement is real. The Metropolitan Streets Act 1867 required London cabs to carry hay for their horses. The horse requirement was removed when the horse was removed. The hay requirement was not. Britain Forward keeps the hay. Why? Because some traditions are worth preserving for the sheer excellence of their absurdity, and because it is technically still the law.)*

---

**On Single Persons Carrying Lengthy Timber**

It is an offence for a single person, acting alone and without assistance, to carry a plank of wood measuring more than one metre in length past more than four separate dwellings, without pausing between each dwelling to rest the plank.

*(Based on the Metropolitan Police Act 1839, which made it an offence to carry a plank along a footway. Updated for metric.)*

---

**On the Impersonation of Extraterrestrial or Undead Creatures**

It is an offence for any person to wilfully impersonate an extraterrestrial being, undead entity, spectral presence, or creature of supernatural origin in a manner likely to cause genuine public alarm or distress.

Performances within licenced theatrical venues, costumed events with prior public notice, and activities within clearly designated entertainment contexts are exempt.

The offence is complete when a reasonable person, encountering the impersonation without prior warning, would experience genuine alarm rather than mild amusement.

*(This provision has been requested by multiple local authorities and at least three post offices.)*

---

**On Speaking to Pigeons**

It is an offence to address a pigeon in a public place with the intent of attracting it toward oneself, offering it food, or establishing any form of ongoing relationship with it.

A person may acknowledge a pigeon in passing. A person may not court one.

*(Pigeon feeding bans exist in numerous cities globally. Ours is merely more personal.)*

---

**On the Impermissibility of Buttering Cats**

It is an offence to apply butter, margarine, vegetable spread, or any other fat-based culinary substance to the fur, skin, or any external surface of a domestic or wild cat.

Veterinary procedures requiring topical application of emollient substances, conducted by or under the supervision of a registered veterinary professional, are exempt.

*(The butter-a-cat law is one of the great whimsical legal traditions. It stays. No further justification is offered or required.)*

## CATEGORY THREE: CIVIC TRADITIONS — THE FIVE JIUANS

*The Jiuans are proposed major national holidays — civic celebrations distinct from existing bank holidays and religious observances. They belong to everyone.*

---

**The Five Jiuans**

Britain Forward proposes five new national civic holidays — the Jiuans — to mark the turning points of the civic year:

**Calan Ganvar** — 1st January
New Year. The beginning of the civic year. A day of new beginnings, public celebration, and collective resolve. Named from the Welsh *Calan* (first day) and *Ganvar* (winter).

**Preidese** — 13th March, or the first Friday after the 13th
The Festival of Light. Marking the turning toward spring. A day of community events, outdoor celebration (weather permitting, this being Britain), and the opening of public spaces for gatherings.

**Samtsirch** — 25th June
Midsummer. The height of the civic year. A day of arts, performance, community, and outdoor life.

**Eser** — 13th September, or the nearest Friday before the 13th
The Harvest Festival. Marking the turning toward winter. A day of appreciation for what the year has produced — agricultural, cultural, and civic.

**Decalan** — 31st December
New Year's Eve. The end of the civic year. Reflection, celebration, and preparation for Calan Ganvar.

---

**Laws Pertaining to the Jiuans**

*On Laundry:*
It is an offence to wash clothes or household textiles on any of the five Jiuans.

*(This is not about laundry. It is about rest. Jiuans are public holidays. Laundry can wait.)*

*On Commercial Theatrical and Dance Venues:*
Theatres and licensed dance schools shall not open for commercial performances on Samtsirch, nor on the thirteenth day of any month.

*(An exception exists for charitable performances and community-organised events not operating for commercial gain.)*

*On UFO Landing:*
It is an offence for any unidentified flying object to land upon the beaches of the West Coast or South Coast of Britain, between the hours of sunrise and sunset, on any day that is not a Jiuan.

On Jiuans, UFO landings on these beaches require prior notification to the relevant Coastal Authority.

*(Britain's beaches are beautiful. The skies are busy enough.)*

## CATEGORY FOUR: GLORIOUSLY, UNAPOLOGETICALLY WEIRD

*No justification is offered. No justification is needed.*

---

**On Persons Born on the Twenty-Ninth of February**

It is an offence to be born on the twenty-ninth of February.

The law acknowledges that the offending party is rarely in a position to prevent this. The offence is therefore non-punishable and carries no penalty. It is simply noted for the record.

Persons born on this date shall be known, in formal civic documentation, as *Leaplings* and shall receive a small official acknowledgement of the inconvenience on each occurrence of the twenty-ninth of February.

---

**On Dying on One's Own Birthday**

It is an offence to die on one's own birthday.

The law further acknowledges that enforcement of this provision is historically challenging. Deceased persons found to have committed this offence shall be noted in the civic register as having done so, and a small asterisk shall appear beside their entry.

No further consequence attaches. The asterisk speaks for itself.

---

**On Noisy Clothing Near Beaches**

It is an offence to wear clothing or footwear that produces audible noise with each step — including but not limited to sequinned garments, items fitted with bells, and footwear with insufficiently secured embellishments — within sight of the sea or within thirty metres of a designated or signed beach.

"Audible noise" means noise detectable at a distance of two metres by a person of standard hearing in conditions of moderate ambient sound.

The provision does not apply to swimmers, who are permitted to jangle as they like.

---

**On Mixed-Sex Swimming Pools**

In any mixed-sex public swimming pool, the southernmost portion of the pool — comprising no less than four-ninths of the total pool area — shall be designated for the use of women.

The northernmost portion of the pool — also comprising no less than four-ninths of the total pool area — shall be designated for the use of men.

The remaining one-ninth of the pool area shall constitute a neutral zone, in which all persons may swim without directional obligation.

Any pool that is wider than it is long shall determine its southernmost and northernmost portions by reference to the orientation of the principal entrance.

Circular pools shall be divided by reference to true north, as determined by compass.

*(This provision was included because of the glorious specificity of the four-ninths. The four-ninths stays.)*

---

**On Skydiving in the Nude**

It is an offence to skydive while unclothed.

An exception exists for any woman who can demonstrate, by documentary evidence submitted to the relevant Aviation Authority at least 28 days in advance, that she is born of a virgin, herself born of a virgin.

Such applications shall be processed in the order received. The Aviation Authority shall establish a dedicated processing department for this purpose.

The department shall be called the Office of Immaculate Aeronautics.

---

**On Green Undergarments**

No person may wear undergarments of a green colour unless they are a provable direct descendant of the Honourable Marija Kapastova.

Proof of descent must be notarised and shall be accepted in all courts, employment tribunals, and swimming pool changing rooms.

The relevant genealogical records of the Kapastova family shall be held by the National Archives.

*(The Honourable Marija Kapastova is a person of considerable historical significance whose descendants are remarkably numerous and yet consistently overlooked.)*

---

**On Goose-Accompanied Persons at Public Fountains**

It is an offence to approach a public drinking fountain on the third of August whilst accompanied by a goose.

Geese are not permitted to drink from public drinking fountains on the third of August. Their owners are similarly restricted.

The provision does not apply to the second or fourth of August, which are unrestricted goose-fountain days.

*(There are no unrestricted goose-fountain days. See the provision on public drinking fountains above.)*

---

**On the Legal Status of Ghosts, Spirits, and Other Spectral Presences**

The law recognises that certain matters may arise in which the participation, testimony, or interests of deceased persons, spirits, or other non-corporeal entities become relevant to legal proceedings.

In such cases, the following principles apply:

a) A credibly attested ghost may provide testimony through a medium licensed under the Spectral Witness Act *(to be enacted)*, subject to the same standards of cross-examination as any other witness
b) The interests of a spectral presence with documented historical connection to a property in dispute may be considered by the court, represented by an appointed advocate
c) No ghost may be compelled to testify against its own interests
d) Ghosts retain the right to silence
e) A haunting shall not be considered adequate grounds for the breaking of a tenancy agreement unless the haunting is continuous, demonstrably non-consensual, and materially interferes with the quiet enjoyment of the premises
f) The question of whether a ghost may bring a claim in its own right shall be determined on a case-by-case basis, having regard to the principles developed under the Nature as Legal Entities framework

*(The Law Commission has been asked to develop guidance. They have not yet responded. The Law Commission's silence is, in itself, legally interesting.)*

# APPENDIX B: THE VOTER COMPETENCE LESSONS AND CIVIC VOTING CENTRES

*A difficult question, addressed honestly.*

---

## The Proposal

Every citizen completes Voter Competence Lessons (VCLs) before the age of 21 (natural-born citizens) or within the first five years of citizenship (naturalised citizens).

The VCL tests civic and constitutional knowledge only — how Parliament works, how votes are counted, how public services function, what rights and responsibilities citizens hold. It does not test ideological positions. It does not test policy preferences. It does not require agreement with any political view.

**Results:**

*Fail:* Standard voting rights in all national and local matters. The franchise is not removed. It is not diminished. A person who fails the VCL votes exactly as they would without it.

*Pass:* Standard voting rights, plus an additional weighted vote at Civic Voting Centres (CVCs) in national matters, counted as one additional vote.

*Commended Pass:* Standard voting rights, plus an additional weighted vote at CVCs counted as five-thirds of an additional vote.

**In practice:**
- A person with a Fail effectively has 1 vote
- A person with a Pass effectively has 2 votes
- A person with a Commended Pass effectively has 2⅔ votes

CVCs count these additional votes only in national matters. Cities, counties, and regional assemblies may determine their own local applications.

---

## The Historical Problem — Stated Honestly

Literacy tests used as voting prerequisites in the American South were used explicitly and systematically to disenfranchise Black voters. This is not a distant theoretical concern — it happened, it was effective, and it destroyed democratic participation for millions of people over decades.

Any mechanism that links civic knowledge to voting weight will be — correctly — assessed against this history.

---

## Why This Is Different — The Distinctions That Matter

**Nobody loses their vote.** Ever. Under any circumstances. A person who fails the VCL votes in every election, every referendum, for everything, exactly as before. There is no disenfranchisement.

**The VCL must be non-prohibitive by statute.** The content is fixed by an independent body with no political appointees. It tests only constitutional and civic facts that are matters of public record and genuinely neutral — the mechanics of how democracy operates, not the ideology of who should win. The test cannot be changed by a government of the day to suit its own voters.

**Free, unlimited retakes.** The VCL is free. It can be taken as many times as a person wishes. There is no time limit after which it expires. Active support is provided for people who struggle.

**Not a prerequisite for participation — an enhancement of it.** The base franchise is identical for everyone. The VCL offers additional civic weight to those who demonstrate additional civic knowledge — it does not subtract from anyone.

**The design is publicly fixed in statute, not administratively determined.** The content, marking criteria, and pass/commended thresholds are set in primary legislation. No minister, no civil servant, and no political party can adjust them between elections.

---

## The Remaining Concern — And Why It Is Taken Seriously

Even with these safeguards, there is a genuine question: who benefits from weighted voting systems in practice?

If civic knowledge correlates with education, and education correlates with socioeconomic background, then a knowledge-weighted voting system may, in practice, amplify the votes of those who already have structural advantages — regardless of the neutrality of the test itself.

This is not a reason to abandon the proposal. It is a reason to ensure that the VCL is genuinely accessible — including through:
- Free provision in schools (all students complete it before leaving)
- Free provision for adults through libraries, job centres, and online
- Active outreach to communities with historically lower civic engagement
- Accommodations for disability, dyslexia, language, and cognitive difference
- Regular audit of pass rates disaggregated by demographic — published annually

If the audit shows systematic demographic disparity in outcomes that cannot be explained by factors other than opportunity, the system is reformed. This commitment is statutory.

---

## The Bottom Line

The VCL is kept because the principle — that informed participation deserves greater weight than uninformed participation — is defensible, provided the information being tested is genuinely neutral and genuinely accessible.

The safeguards — non-prohibitive design, unlimited retakes, no disenfranchisement, independent body, statutory content, demographic audit — are not optional. They are the thing that makes the policy different from its historical misuse.

If any safeguard is removed, the policy falls. The safeguards are the policy.

# APPENDIX C: ENTRUSTED PERSONS AND CRIMINAL PENALTIES

*On the heightened responsibility of those who hold public trust.*

---

## The Principle

A person who holds a position of public trust — who is given authority, recognition, rights, and responsibilities by the public, on behalf of the public — and who then commits a crime, has not merely broken the law. They have betrayed the trust that the public placed in them.

This betrayal is a distinct harm, separate from the harm of the crime itself.

It warrants a distinct response.

---

## Who Is an Entrusted Person?

An entrusted person is anyone who holds a position of recognised public authority or responsibility, including:

- Members of the Armed Forces (serving or recently separated)
- Police officers and law enforcement personnel
- Medical professionals in patient-facing roles
- Teachers, educators, and youth workers
- Social workers and care workers
- Members of Parliament, councillors, and public officials
- Judges and court officers
- Prison and probation officers
- Any person whose role involves authority over, or care for, vulnerable individuals

---

## The Two Tiers of Consequence

**Tier One: Loss of Status and Benefits**

Any entrusted person convicted of a criminal offence that:
a) Was made possible by their position of trust, or
b) Involved the exploitation of their authority, access, or relationship with those in their care, or
c) Represents a fundamental betrayal of the values their position required them to uphold

...shall forfeit the recognition, awards, honorary titles, and professional standing associated with that position.

For members of the Armed Forces specifically: conviction for a crime involving betrayal of the values of service — violence against civilians, abuse of authority, exploitation of those under their protection — results in:
- De-recognition of rank for the purpose of the crime and subsequently
- Forfeiture of service-related awards and decorations
- Loss of pension entitlements earned after the conviction
- Loss of the right to claim the protections and privileges of veteran status in the context of criminal proceedings

*This is not additional punishment. It is the removal of recognition that is no longer merited.*

Having once served does not immunise a person from the law. Having once served does not excuse a crime. Having once served and then committed a crime that betrays the values of service means, specifically, that the service record and its associated recognition are no longer a complete picture of who this person is.

The recognition was given in trust. The trust was broken. The recognition goes with it.

---

**Tier Two: Enhanced Sentencing for Abuse of Trust**

Where a crime was enabled by, or committed through, an entrusted position, the sentencing guidelines apply an enhancement.

This enhancement applies to:
- Crimes committed against persons in the entrusted person's care or authority
- Crimes using equipment, access, or information obtained through the position
- Crimes that exploited the psychological authority of the position (e.g. a person complying because they trusted the authority figure)

The enhancement is proportionate to the degree to which the position enabled the crime — not simply because the person once held the position, but because the position was instrumentally used in the commission of the crime.

*A former soldier who shoplifts: standard sentencing. No enhancement.*
*A former soldier who uses combat training to assault someone: enhancement applies — the position contributed directly to the crime.*
*A doctor who abuses a patient: maximum enhancement — the position entirely enabled and facilitated the crime.*

---

## On Ex-Soldiers Specifically

Britain Forward respects and honours the service of those who have served in the Armed Forces. That respect is not unlimited. It does not extend to immunity from consequences.

Some individuals who have served appear to believe that service confers a special status above the law, or that courts and the public will be reluctant to hold them accountable. They are wrong. They will be held accountable. In some cases — where the crime specifically betrays the values of service — they will be held more accountable, not less.

The law applies to everyone. The trust that comes with public service is honourable precisely because it is not unconditional. Betray it, and the law responds accordingly.
