**The tagline:** Not Left. Not Right. Forward.

**The ideology:** Sovereign Democracy — Britain sovereign over its own resources, decisions, and future. Evidence-based policy, not tribal ideology. Honest about trade-offs.

**The honest positioning:** The platform acknowledges it leans left in some areas (nationalisation, UBI, universal services) and right in others (strong borders, business innovation, national self-sufficiency). It takes what works from wherever it comes. Evidence, not tribe.

## KEY STRUCTURAL FEATURES (unique to Britain Forward)

**Vote of Incompetence:** Leader or senior member who fails their role: step down or be removed. Five governance requirements including independent performance panel and two-thirds NEC supermajority to initiate. Political disagreement is NOT grounds.

**Office for Evidence-Based Policy:** Independent body reporting to Parliament. Publishes all research in full — technical AND plain English simultaneously. Government cannot suppress findings.

**Plain Language Commissioner:** Independent office. Can fine public bodies that fail the plain language standard. Annual public compliance report.

**Three-tier nationalisation:** Not the 1970s. Public networks (100% owned); majority public operating companies with citizen shares; fully private competitive services on top. Acquisition through RAB model and long-term bonds, self-funded from revenues.

**Anti-fragmentation rules for small business tax:** Five legal tests, any one of which triggers common control assessment. Burden of proof on the business. 100% penalty plus criminal referral for deliberate fragmentation.

**Automatic fuel duty relief trigger:** Both conditions must be met simultaneously — a qualifying crisis event (defined list) AND price threshold (20% above 36-month rolling average for 14+ days). No ministerial decision required.

**Language Competency Standard:** Universal — applies to everyone including native Brits. British citizens cannot be deported if they fail (constitutionally impossible); they face mandatory education and retest. The political judo: Far Right people who write "we want are cuntry back" fail the same test as every immigrant.

## STYLE AND TONE NOTES

**Voice:** Direct. Plain English. Evidence-cited. Honest about what isn't known yet. Never preachy. Acknowledges trade-offs rather than pretending they don't exist.

**What Britain Forward will NOT do:**
- Pretend everything is affordable without showing the working
- Hide controversial policies (they're stated openly with criteria for action)
- Flip-flop under media pressure
- Lead with deferred policies (drugs, sex work, relationship law, nudity) in campaign materials
- Claim to be purely centrist (honestly left-leaning in some areas, right-leaning in others)

**What Britain Forward WILL do:**
- Show its working — always, in both technical and plain English
- Reverse policies when evidence shows they're failing
- Acknowledge what it doesn't yet know (Open Placeholders)
- Apply the same standards to itself that it applies to others
