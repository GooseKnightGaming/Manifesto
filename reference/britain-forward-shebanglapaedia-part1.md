# WHO WE ARE

## Not Left. Not Right. Forward.

**Not Left** — we don't accept that helping people must come at the structural expense of the businesses they depend on. Where trade-offs exist, we acknowledge them honestly and minimise them visibly.

**Not Right** — we don't accept that helping business must come at the structural expense of the people businesses serve. Where trade-offs exist, we acknowledge them honestly and minimise them visibly.

**Not Centre** — we reject the triangulated preservation of a status quo that is demonstrably not working. "Moderate" is not the same as "right." Avoiding hard choices is not wisdom. It is failure deferred.

**Forward** — for every policy, we ask: does this make Britain genuinely better? Trade-offs are acknowledged openly. Mitigations are planned visibly. Evidence, not tribe, guides us. Always.

Some of our policies look left. Some look right. Most look like neither — because they come from evidence, not ideology. We take what works from wherever it comes. We leave what doesn't. We show our working.

**Evidence, not tribe. Honesty, not pretence. Always.**

# PILLAR ONE: FIX THE SYSTEM
## Democratic & Political Renewal

---

## 1.1 Ministerial Competence

Ministers will have demonstrable expertise or professional experience relevant to their department.

Health Secretary: worked in healthcare.
Chancellor: worked in economics.
Education Secretary: worked in education.
Defence Secretary: served or worked in defence.

This is not elitism. This is responsibility. No learning on the job at the nation's expense.

---

## 1.2 Electoral Reform — Ranked Weighted Voting

Ranked Weighted Voting replaces First Past the Post. Voters rank candidates in preference order. Points awarded by ranking. Winners must command broad support — not just an intense minority. No wasted votes. No tactical voting. Every constituency matters.

**Compulsory Participation — Australian Model.** Every eligible citizen participates. Spoiled ballots and None of the Above are valid choices.

*HRA fallback:* If legal opinion concludes compulsory voting is unlawful under the Human Rights Act, we implement an incentive-based alternative. The principle — full democratic participation — is preserved; the mechanism adapts to constitutional law.

**None of the Above on every ballot.** Formally counted, published, acted on. NOTA winning outright triggers fresh election within 60 days — original candidates ineligible. Caretaker from National Oversight Council serves until completion.

**Coalition Red Lines — stated before any election:**
Britain Forward will not coalition with any party that:
- Opposes ranked weighted voting
- Reverses public ownership of core infrastructure
- Abandons ministerial competence requirements
Everything else is negotiable. These three are not.

---

## 1.3 Policy-Based Voting — The Long-Term Direction

Most voters want policies from multiple parties — not one party's entire package. The current system forces all-or-nothing choices.

**The Swiss Federal Council Model:** A National Implementation Council of five to seven senior figures drawn proportionally from parties per electoral share, confirmed by Parliament through cross-party vote, serving fixed terms. The Council implements. Parliament debates and legislates. Citizens vote directly on major questions through binding referenda.

This separates three currently collapsed functions: democratic mandate, legislative process, and executive implementation.

**Britain Forward's pathway:**
- This Parliament: Citizens' Assembly established; policy-based voting researched and published
- Next Parliament: Binding referendum on Assembly proposals
- Long-term: Constitutional framework adapted based on what Britain votes for

This is too significant to impose. Britain must vote on it.

---

## 1.4 Constitutional Framework

**The Monarchy — Constitutional Custodian of Last Resort.** Sovereignty resides in the British people. The Monarch is a non-political safeguard acting only in genuine constitutional crisis to restore conditions for popular sovereignty. Largely ceremonial. Present and real in extremis.

**The House of Lords — Public Decision.** Current form: indefensible. Function: valuable. Three reform options published; public consultation; binding referendum. The public decides. Options: elected Senate; hybrid chamber (partially elected, partially independently expert-appointed); full abolition with functions to National Oversight Council.

**National Oversight Council.** Mixed composition — elected members plus independently appointed experts. No government appointments, ever. Power to delay and refer legislation. Integrated with judicial review.

**Parliament — London Now, Direction Set.** Government departments: phased regional relocation begins immediately. Independent commission assesses Parliament's future location — reports within five years. Public referendum decides.

---

## 1.5 Political Accountability

**Conflict of Interest — The Precise Standard.**

The test: Did this company materially benefit from a specific decision this MP made or influenced?

- No involvement with such companies in the previous 5 years before taking office
- No involvement while serving
- No involvement for 10 years after leaving office
- Where demonstrable benefit flows to an MP through a family connection: same standard applies

Sector expertise: encouraged. Personal financial benefit from specific decisions: prohibited.

**MP Pay — No Floor. No Exceptions.**

MPs are paid the median wage of the constituency they represent. Full stop. No floor above the constituency reality. The principle is skin-in-the-game: fix it for your constituency, or live with what you've failed to fix.

Reasonable accommodation and travel costs covered for genuine work purposes. Zero outside income while serving. All expenses: publicly searchable, audited quarterly. Personal and family costs never eligible. Pay rises: same process as public sector — not self-determined.

---

## 1.6 Transparent Tax Spending

Annual plain-English breakdown — national and local. Every library, GP surgery, job centre, post office, and online. Independently verified. Actual proportions.

The reason people resist taxation is not selfishness — it's that they don't see the return. France and Scandinavia have higher tax rates and higher public acceptance because the benefit is visible. Britain will do the same.

---

## 1.7 Public Budget Reform — End "Use It Or Lose It"

Public bodies that underspend risk having their budget cut next year. The rational response: spend everything, whether it needs spending or not. This incentivises waste and punishes efficiency.

**Five Fixes — Applied to All Public Bodies:**
1. Multi-Year Budgets — three-year rolling settlements
2. Carry-Forward Allowance — up to 20% rolls forward to next year
3. Efficiency Dividend — genuine savings: 50% retained by the organisation, 50% returns to central budget
4. Outcome-Based Funding Review — based on demonstrated need and achieved outcomes, not last year's spend
5. Ring-Fenced Honest Underspend — organisations reporting honestly are protected from automatic cuts

---

## 1.8 Plain Language — A Legal Standard

All government communications must meet the Plain Language Standard before issue.

**The Standard:**
- Reading age: accessible to approximately age 12 for standard communications; age 9 for emergency communications
- Average sentence length under 20 words; no sentence over 35 words
- One idea per paragraph
- Jargon defined on first use; avoided where plain alternatives exist
- Active, direct address: "you need to do X by Y date" — not "it is required that the recipient comply"
- Every communication states: what you need to do, by when, and what happens if you don't

**The Plain Language Commissioner:** Independent office. Powers to audit, require rewriting, and fine bodies that systematically fail. Reports to Parliament, not ministers.

**Plain Language in Courts:** Every participant in legal proceedings has the right to a plain-English explanation of what is happening, what is being decided, and what their options are.

**Britain Forward applies this to itself first.** If something cannot be explained plainly, it has not been thought through clearly enough.

> *"If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough."*

## 1.9 Civil Service Reform

Outcome-based accountability. Digital-first government. Cross-department integration. Major projects through independent delivery bodies — engineering decisions by engineers, not politicians. Political neutrality protected.

## 1.10 Federal Structure — The Canton Principle for Britain

**The Principle — Subsidiarity:**

Decisions should be made at the lowest level of government that can make them effectively. National government should not decide what a village does with its high street.

**The England Problem:**

Switzerland's largest canton has ~16% of national population. England has 85% of the UK population. A cantonal model treating England as one unit reproduces the existing problem. The answer: English regional assemblies with real powers.

**The Four-Level Federal Model:**

*Level One — Federal Government:* Defence, foreign policy, macro-economic policy, currency, immigration, constitutional matters, national infrastructure standards.

*Level Two — National Parliaments:*
- Scotland: Scottish Parliament — powers maintained
- Wales: Senedd — powers expanded to match Scottish model
- Northern Ireland: Assembly — maintained under Good Friday Agreement
- England: NEW English Parliament or Assembly — separate from UK Parliament; ends the West Lothian Question

*Level Three — English Regional Assemblies (approximately 9 regions):*
Greater London (GLA expanded) | South East | South West | East of England | East Midlands | West Midlands | Yorkshire and the Humber | North West | North East

Regional powers: transport, skills, economic development, spatial planning, healthcare delivery, policing priorities, culture and tourism.

*Level Four — Local Government:*
Day-to-day local services, local planning, local transport, parks, environmental enforcement, local housing decisions.

**Fiscal Model:** Each level has defined taxing powers within federal limits. Fiscal equalisation mechanism: richer regions contribute to a fund supporting less well-resourced regions — formula-based, transparent, not politically negotiated.

**Implementation — Generational:**
- This Parliament: Constitutional Convention established; federal model designed; public consultation; draft framework published
- Referendum: UK-wide vote on whether to proceed
- Next Parliament (if yes): Enabling legislation; English regional assemblies established
- Subsequent Parliaments: Full implementation over 10-15 years

Britain decides. Not us.

## 2.1 The Sovereign Wealth Fund

Built from: nationalised asset revenues; pharmaceutical savings; stopped mismanagement; British oil surplus; UBI higher-earner tax recoupture; foreign aid redirected from ineffective programmes; budget reform savings; industrial growth as small businesses scale.

Capital investment financed through long-term bonds — repaid by the assets' own revenues. We are building Norway's model, starting now. Every year we fail to start is compounded loss.

## 2.2 Nationalisation — Three-Tier Model

**Why:** Water, energy networks, and oil are natural monopolies or strategic national resources. Every comparable successful economy maintains public ownership or strong public control of core infrastructure. Britain is the outlier.

**Tier One — Physical Networks: 100% Publicly Owned.**
British Water. British Grid. British Rail Infrastructure.
One body. Direct accountability. Answerable to Parliament. When water is contaminated — British Water is accountable. No chain of private company, internal review, regulator, tribunal. Direct. Real consequences.

*Acquisition:* Regulatory Asset Base model — asset value, not inflated market valuations. Long-term bond financing. Self-funded from asset revenues. Distressed companies first (Thames Water is effectively insolvent — this is an opportunity, not a tragedy). Legal opinions published before any legislation. 5-7 year operational transition per sector.

**Tier Two — Operating Companies: Majority Public, Citizen Shares.**
51%+ government. Up to 49% to British citizens directly — dividends, shareholder voice, genuine stake in their country. Some market discipline maintained.

**Tier Three — Services: Fully Private, Fully Competitive.**
The internet model. Public infrastructure. Private innovation on top.

**This is not the 1970s.** The 1970s failed because of: political interference in operational decisions; union capture; soft budget constraints removing efficiency incentive; no competition at any level. Our model avoids all of these. The honest comparisons: Norwegian Equinor, French EDF, German Deutsche Bahn. Every successful European economy has this. Britain is the outlier.

## 2.3 Small Business — Britain's Best Place to Start

**The Vision:** Near-zero tax for genuine small enterprises. Britain becomes the best place in the world to start something.

| Annual Qualifying Revenue | Corporation Tax Rate |
|---|---|
| Under £100,000 | 0% — zero |
| £100,000 — £500,000 | Minimal (target: 5%) |
| £500,000 — £5 million | Intermediate |
| £5 million — £50 million | Standard |
| Over £50 million | Full — no reduction |

**The Anti-Fragmentation Rule — Five Legal Tests (any one triggers common control assessment):**
1. One entity holds more than 25% beneficial ownership in two or more companies
2. A director or officer holds executive authority in two or more companies
3. Two or more companies share premises, branding, supply chains, customer base, or key personnel above a defined threshold
4. Non-arm's-length transactions between companies at demonstrably artificial rates
5. A company divided after this legislation — presumed single entity for 10 years unless genuine independence demonstrated to HMRC's satisfaction

Burden of proof on the business. Deliberate fragmentation: full back-tax plus 100% penalty plus interest plus criminal referral.

*Additional:* Simplified reporting; business rates relief for small high-street businesses; late payment penalties for large companies; public procurement reserved for small businesses below a defined threshold.

## 2.5 Fuel Duty Relief — Automatic and Defined

**Both conditions must be met simultaneously:**

*Condition 1 — Qualifying Crisis Event (confirmed by independent assessment):*
- Armed conflict directly affecting a major oil-producing region or supply route
- OPEC+ production cut of more than 10% of quota within a 90-day period
- Sanctions on a major producer affecting more than 5% of global supply
- Critical infrastructure failure affecting more than 5% of European refining or distribution capacity
- A declared energy emergency by the International Energy Agency

*Petrol being 6p more expensive than last week does not trigger this. War in the Middle East disrupting global supply does.*

*Condition 2 — Price Threshold:* Retail unleaded petrol exceeds the 36-month rolling average by more than 20%, sustained for at least 14 consecutive days.

Relief: up to 50% of standard rate, scaling with severity. Ends automatically when either condition lapses — assessed every 14 days. No ministerial decision required. No politics.

## 2.7 Universal Basic Income

**Target rate:** ~£100/week. Confirmed by pilot evidence and independent modelling before national rollout.

**Who qualifies:**

| Status | UBI | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| British citizens | Full | The baseline |
| Settled residents (5+ years) | Full | Contribution threshold met |
| High-skilled visa holders | Full after 2 years | Shorter threshold: economic contribution recognised |
| Standard visa holders | Full after 5 years | Standard threshold |
| Pensioners | Full UBI + pension | Pension is a separate entitlement |
| Working people | Full UBI + wages | UBI supplements, not replaces |
| Job-seeking unemployed | Full UBI + support | Active employment support alongside |
| Non-seeking non-disabled unemployed | Full UBI only | Their choice. £100/week is their life. |
| Disabled people | Full UBI + disability support | Disability support is separate, needs-based |
| Under criminal sentence | Suspended | Resumes on day of release |
| Foreign nationals without qualifying residency | Not eligible | — |

**On the "laziness" objection:** Evidence from Finland, Kenya, Canada, and Alaska consistently shows UBI increases workforce participation. People in jobs they chose, that they can afford to take, stay longer and perform better.

**Net cost:** Independent modelling required before national rollout. Gross figure (~£260bn) is not the relevant number — tax recoupture from higher earners and benefits system replacement savings substantially reduce it.

**Implementation:** Pilots this Parliament. National rollout decision next Parliament, based on evidence — published in full, technical and plain English.

## 2.11 Economic Stability Architecture

## 3.1 Roads — Built to Last

Long-life composite surfaces — built to last decades, not years. Whole-life costing in all contracts — cheapest upfront is never cheapest overall. Road-embedded EV charging — inductive coils during rebuild; laden commercial EVs viable. Green corridors alongside all new roads. Usage-based road pricing replaces fuel duty as EVs grow — fair, transparent, hypothecated to roads and transport.

## 3.2 Public Dignity Infrastructure

*Not charity. Infrastructure.*

**Public Toilets:** Statutory minimum, ring-fenced funding. All A-road laybys: free, maintained, lit, safe. HGV rest areas: proper facilities, showers, security, CCTV, on-site security on high-theft routes. Petrol stations on public contracts: free access required. Changing Places in all new major public buildings. Free at point of use — 30p turnstile abolished. National standards inspected and enforced.

Where hand washing facilities are available, they must be: functional, supplied with soap, supplied with running warm water, and supplied with a drying method. A publicly maintained toilet without functioning hand washing facilities is not compliant.

**Public Showers:** At community centres, leisure facilities, and urban hubs. Infrastructure, not charity.

**Public Kitchens:** Accessible cooking spaces, any time, FSA-standard, staffed during peak hours. Evening cooking classes. Available to anyone.

**Public Orchards & Community Growing:** Public orchards in parks — free harvest. Commons Reserve — protected seed stock, clearly signed, not for harvest. Community growing alongside. Connected to schools.

## 3.3 Public Transport — Free or Heavily Subsidised

**Buses — Free at Point of Use:** Urban, suburban, and rural. Minimum service standards — 30-minute peak frequency baseline. Night services restored. Real-time information at all stops.

**Trams:** Inner-city tram networks in all major cities above a defined population threshold. Extensions to existing systems. New systems assessed for cities where density supports viability. Free or heavily subsidised fares.

**Urban Commuter Rail:** Fares capped and subsidised within major city regions. Integrated ticketing. Oyster-style systems extended to all major city regions.

## 3.4 Walkable Cities — The 15-Minute Principle

The most liveable cities have most daily needs accessible within 15 minutes on foot. Britain Forward adopts this as the design standard for all new development.

New developments: maximum distances — primary school (10-min walk), GP (10-min walk), green space (5-min walk), shops (10-min walk). Street design prioritises pedestrian movement. Ground floors: shops, cafes, services — not car parks or blank walls. Continuous, well-lit, accessible pavements. Pavement parking: enforcement substantially strengthened — pavements are for people, not car storage.

---

## 3.5 Bikeable Cities — Dutch Model

Physical separation, not painted lines. Protected cycle lanes on all urban arterial roads — raised kerb, planters, or parking buffer. Minimum 1.5m each direction. Continuous — a lane that disappears at every junction is not a lane. Separate cyclist signal phases at junctions. Secure covered cycle parking everywhere. Publicly funded bike-share schemes in all cities above a defined threshold. E-bike subsidy for lower-income households.

## 3.6 Energy — Britain Powers Britain

**Honest Transition Sequence:** Renewable capacity cannot replace all demand immediately. During the transition, Britain maintains domestic fossil fuel production. This is not a contradiction — it is honest sequencing. Germany's premature nuclear phase-out and dependence on Russian gas is the cautionary tale.

**Renewables:** Solar (rooftops not fields, mandatory on all new builds); Wind (onshore and offshore); Tidal and wave (world-class British resources — currently strategic negligence); Hydro (Scotland's river network, community micro-hydro); Geothermal (Cornwall as expansion template).

**Nuclear:** Stable baseload. Next-generation reactor investment.

**Transition Fuels — Milestone Phase-Out (achievement thresholds, not calendar dates):**
- Coal power: phases out when green baseload can cover peak demand for 30 consecutive days
- Gas for domestic heating: phases out sector by sector as heat pump penetration and grid capacity demonstrate readiness
- Coking coal: phases out when green steel is commercially viable at scale in Britain

**Worker Transition — Explicit Commitment:** Workers in oil, gas, and coal not abandoned. Retraining funded. Green energy jobs prioritised for former fossil fuel workers. No community left behind. Policy, not aspiration.

Gigabit broadband universal — publicly funded where commercially unviable. Satellite broadband as interim. Mobile healthcare. Telemedicine. Rural pharmacies and post offices: protected.

## 3.8 The National High-Speed Rail Spine
*Bournemouth to Thurso — Britain to Britain*

Not a London-to-somewhere line. A Britain-to-Britain line. London is a stop along the way, not the destination everything radiates from.

**The Spine:** Bournemouth → Southampton → London junction → Milton Keynes → Birmingham → Manchester → Leeds → Newcastle → Edinburgh → Inverness → Thurso

**The Branches:** Southwest (Plymouth, Cornwall); Wales (Cardiff, Swansea); East (Norwich, East Anglia); Yorkshire (Hull, York); Northeast (Sunderland, Middlesbrough); Northeast Scotland (Aberdeen, Dundee); Northwest Scotland (Fort William)

Thurso already has a railway — the Far North Line. North of Inverness is speed upgrade and reliability improvement, not entirely new track. The communities most disconnected from national infrastructure are explicitly included.

**Engineering Principles:** Independent delivery body. Engineering decisions by engineers. Routes by economic benefit analysis. Transparent budget with genuine contingency published before construction approved. Multi-Parliament direction — will not be cancelled when inconvenient.

HS2 failed because of political interference, not engineering impossibility. The lesson is how not to do it — not whether to do it.

## 3.12 International Green Pressure

**Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM):** Imports from countries not meeting agreed minimum carbon standards face a border levy proportionate to the carbon cost of production not priced in the import. WTO compliant. The EU has already implemented this.

**Trade Agreements:** All new bilateral agreements include binding green standards — not aspirational language.

**International Carbon Accountability Report:** Published annually. Countries backing out of commitments face: public naming, CBAM at full rate, reduced partnership investment priority. Agreements mean something. Britain acts like they do.

## 4.2 Mental Health — Enforced Parity, Not Symbolic Parity

Parity of esteem between physical and mental health has been legally required in the NHS for over a decade. It has not been delivered. Legal parity without enforcement is symbolism.

**What enforced parity means:**
- Same proportional funding as physical health — measurable, published
- Waiting time targets equivalent to physical health — published, enforced

**The connection:** Homelessness, poverty, job insecurity, and housing instability cause mental health crises. Britain Forward's mental health policy is: fund the services AND address the causes.

## 4.3 Homelessness — Housing First

*Model: Finland (near-zero rough sleeping since 2008); Canada*

Give people a stable home first, unconditionally. Then provide support. Sobriety is not a prerequisite. Mental health stability is not a prerequisite. You cannot address addiction from a park bench. From a stable home, you can. Finland proves this.

**Support alongside housing:** Mental health support; addiction support (voluntary, non-coercive); employment support (patient, individually tailored); benefits navigation.

**Prison release:** Housing plan in place before release date. No one released into homelessness.

**The Target:** Rough sleeping eliminated within one Parliament.

## 4.10 Justice & Criminal Reform

**Principle: Rehabilitation, Not Only Punishment**

Norway's recidivism rate (~20%) vs UK (~50%+). Rehabilitation is more effective AND cheaper. Evidence-led. Not soft — strategic.

**In Prison:** Mental health assessment on entry. Addiction services in prison and on release — continuity, not cliff-edge. Literacy and education. Housing plan before release. In-prison work: meaningful, paid, skills-developing.

**Courts — Faster Justice:** Court capacity expanded. Victim-focused reform throughout. Legal aid restored.

**Policies Under Evidence Review — Justice:**

*Chemical and surgical castration:* Not currently supported. Evidence is methodologically weak; irreversible interventions fail the reversibility requirement. Genuine openness if proper evidence develops. Britain Forward supports instead: forensic mental health treatment; CBT programmes with demonstrated recidivism reduction; appropriately long sentences; robust victim support.

## 5.5 Employment Reform

**Advertised Salaries: Mandatory.** All job ads state actual salary. "Competitive" and "DOE" banned. Salary question in interviews abolished.

**Banned Interview Questions:** Hypothetical personality questions; personal life, family, relationship status; salary history; questions irrelevant to job performance.

**Alternative Assessment Formats:** Work trials, portfolio reviews, task assessments. Public sector leads.

**Neurodivergent Adaptations and Strengths:** Questions in advance as standard option. Reasonable adjustments enforced. Neurodivergent people are a talent pool with specific capabilities — pattern recognition, attention to detail, hyperfocus, systemised thinking. Public sector leads in matching people to roles where their strengths genuinely excel.

**Gait and Physical Bearing Bias:** Assessors make rapid unconscious judgements based on how candidates physically present — including walk and bearing — before any substantive interaction. Where physical presentation is influenced by factors unrelated to job performance (including structural foot differences, footwear design, disability, chronic pain), assessments based on it are both unfair and poor predictors of capability. Gait and physical bearing bias included in unconscious bias training for all public sector hiring. Alternative entry formats available. Office for Evidence-Based Policy commissioned to research foot structure, footwear design, and gait in professional contexts.

**Entry-Level Standards:** Experience requirements for starter positions banned. Employers cannot reclassify entry-level roles to impose experience barriers.

**4-Day Working Week — Flexible Rolling Structure:** Piloted in public sector with rigorous evaluation. Pattern flexibility is the principle — not "everyone gets Friday off." Workers choose their 4-day pattern where jobs permit. Rolling schedules accommodated. Services requiring 7-day coverage through rotating staff patterns, not 5-day individuals.

**Workplace Rights:** Equal pay regardless of age. Zero-hours reform: guaranteed minimum hours for regular workers. Right to disconnect. Corporate pay ratio limits.

## 5.8 National Civic Resilience
*Model: Finland's Comprehensive Security doctrine*

**The Finnish Insight:** An informed citizenry is a component of national defence — not metaphorically, but structurally. A democracy whose citizens can recognise manipulation is harder to destabilise. When a wannabe-authoritarian arrives in a country with genuine civic education, they find an audience that laughs at them.

**The Programme:**

*Propaganda Recognition — Explicit Curriculum:*
How propaganda techniques work: emotional manipulation, false urgency, us-vs-them framing, repetition, manufactured consensus, authority figures, scapegoating. Historical case studies. Modern disinformation: social media exploitation, coordinated inauthentic behaviour, bot-amplified content. Practical application: students assess real historical examples.

*Prebunking:* Teaching the technique before exposure is more effective than correcting after. Recognition before encounter.

*How Democracies Erode — Historical Curriculum:*
Case studies: Weimar Germany, Hungary post-2010, interwar Italy. Common patterns: emergency powers creep, judicial capture, press freedom erosion, minority scapegoating. Early warning signs. What individuals and institutions did — and didn't do — that made a difference.

**The Civic Resilience Standard:** Every student completing compulsory education can identify at least six common manipulation techniques, explain how at least two democratic systems eroded, assess political communications for manipulation indicators, and explain how social media algorithms create filter bubbles.

These are analytical skills — not a political position. They apply equally to manipulation from left, right, foreign governments, advertisers, and Britain Forward.

## 5.9 Language Competency Standard

**The Principle:** Functional communication in a native British language is a reasonable expectation of participation in British civic and economic life.

**This standard applies to everyone** — including native Britons. Because a standard that applies only to immigrants is both discriminatory and immediately circumventable. The people who write "we want are cuntry back" are subject to the same standard as every person they wish to exclude. A universal standard, honestly applied, exposes this.

**Languages accepted:** English; Welsh; Scottish Gaelic; Irish (Northern Ireland); Scots; Cornish; British Sign Language — full equivalence.

**Not tested:** Spelling perfection; academic grammar; vocabulary breadth; accent or pronunciation; regional dialect.

**Tested:** Reading and understanding a short plain-English text. Writing a short response communicating a clear meaning. Understanding simple spoken instructions. Making yourself understood on everyday topics.

**Accommodations — Non-Negotiable:** Dyslexia: extended time, oral alternatives, spelling errors not penalised where meaning is clear. Cognitive impairment: medical assessment pathway; certified by independent panel; exemption or adapted assessment. Physical disability: AAC devices accepted, BSL pathway. Acquired conditions: medical pathway; reassessment after rehabilitation. Recent arrivals: minimum 24 months before assessment; free language education provided.

**Consequences — Honest and Constitutional:**

British citizens cannot be deported — this is constitutionally absolute. Consequence for a citizen who fails: mandatory funded language programme; retest available after programme completion. The political consequence — having failed a standard they claim others should meet — is its own result.

Non-citizens without settled status: failure results in visa application refused or renewal refused. Grace period for genuine recent arrivals.

**Administration:** Libraries, job centres, adult education centres, and online. Free at point of use. No limit on retests. Independent assessment body.

## 6.2 Immigration — Points-Based, Skills-First

*Model: Australia — adapted for British conditions*

**Points-Based Assessment — All Economic Immigration:**

| Factor | Points Available |
|---|---|
| Skills and qualifications (shortage occupations) | Up to 30 |
| English language proficiency (tested) | Up to 20 |
| Age (working-age weighted) | Up to 15 |
| British employer sponsorship (confirmed job offer) | Up to 25 |
| Regional sponsorship (willing to work outside major cities) | Up to 10 |

**Visa Categories:** Skilled Worker (points threshold, employer sponsorship, shortage occupation or salary threshold); High-Value Investor/Entrepreneur (genuine high threshold); Family Reunion (spouse/partner and dependent children of settled residents only); Humanitarian Protection (separate from economic migration, processed fairly, promptly, and humanely).

**UBI and Voting:** 5 years settled residency (2 years for high-skilled visa holders).

## 6.3 Foreign Aid — Reform, Not Sentiment

Independent audit commissioned day one. Three outcome categories:

1. **Retain and expand:** Clear measurable outcomes, genuine recipient benefit — vaccines, emergency relief, demonstrated infrastructure impact
2. **Reform:** Genuine potential, poor implementation — results-based model with measurable outcomes required before continued funding
3. **Cut:** Cannot demonstrate recipient benefit; money captured by corruption or administration; serves donor rather than recipient interests

Britain is not obligated to fund corruption. We are obligated to help where help is effective.

**The Philosophy Underlying Effective Aid:** The next person who could cure a disease, solve a food crisis, or transform clean energy might currently be a child without clean water, without education, without the chance to develop what they have. Effective aid is unlocking capability the world needs — not charity, but self-interest properly understood. This guides which aid Britain retains: not the aid that makes donors feel better, but the aid that genuinely changes what a person can become.

**Results-Based International Partnership:** Trade, science, infrastructure partnerships with measurable outcomes. Britain as a hub of excellence: medicine, food technology, clean energy, engineering, AI. Partnerships of equals — not post-imperial paternalism.

**The EU Question:** Explicit neutrality — not re-litigating Brexit in either direction. Constructive partnership on science, research, education, and trade where agreements serve Britain. Any formal relationship change: public referendum. Britain decides.

# SOCIAL POLICY

## Equality & Identity

Full legal recognition of non-binary identities. Gender transition recognition — streamlined, respectful processes. Protection from discrimination under law.

## Relationships & Unions — Recognition For All

Britain recognises committed adult relationships in all forms. Civil marriage, civil partnership, religious marriage in any faith, pagan hand-fasting, humanist ceremony, secular declaration — all equally valid in law where freely entered between consenting adults.

Universal minimum requirements: consent, adulthood, competence, non-coercion — applied universally regardless of number of participants or tradition.

Where adult relationships extend beyond the traditional two-person form and all parties freely consent, legal recognition is not categorically refused. The core requirements apply universally. Practical legal framework design (inheritance, next-of-kin, parental rights, tax, property) follows evidence-led review with non-negotiable safeguards against coercion.

BSL promoted nationally. Required in healthcare, education, and emergency services. Available as curriculum option in all schools.

## Separation of Church and State

Legal recognition of relationships determined solely by civil law. Religious institutions perform ceremonies freely — no independent legal authority over civil status.

## Nature as Legal Entities

Certain features of the natural world — rivers, ancient forests, coastlines, ecosystems of sufficient complexity and irreplaceability — may be recognised as legal entities capable of having rights defended on their behalf in court.

This is not a fringe concept. New Zealand gave the Whanganui River legal personhood in 2017. The river can sue those who harm it, through appointed guardians. Britain Forward supports a framework for recognising similar legal standing for defined natural entities in Britain, as a mechanism for environmental protection that operates independently of government willingness to prosecute.

# FISCAL FRAMEWORK — THE HONEST VERSION

**Revenue spending** (NHS, UBI, welfare, education, public services): funded from tax revenues and nationalised asset revenues. Recurring costs from recurring income.

**Capital investment** (roads, rail, cities, water, energy): financed through long-term bonds, repaid by the assets' own revenues over their operational lifetime. These are investments, not spending — they generate returns and savings over decades.

*These are not the same thing. Treating infrastructure investment as current spending is how governments make bad decisions.*

**How we pay for it:**
1. Nationalised asset revenues — profits flowing abroad stay in Britain
2. Pharmaceutical savings — we stop overpaying for drugs
3. Stopped mismanagement — procurement reform; benefits administration replaced by UBI efficiency; anti-avoidance enforcement
4. Road pricing — replaces fuel duty fairly as EVs grow
5. UBI tax recoupture — higher earners return their UBI through income tax
6. Foreign aid reform — ineffective programmes redirected
7. Budget reform savings — "use it or lose it" waste eliminated
8. Long-term economic growth from investment in people and infrastructure
9. Long-term bond financing for capital investment, self-funded by asset revenues
10. Small business growth — more businesses, more employment, more income tax as they scale
