# A NOTE ON WHERE WE STAND — HONEST ABOUT WHAT WE ARE

Britain Forward acknowledges something most parties won't:

Our policies draw from multiple traditions. Some look left. Some look right. Most look like neither, because they're drawn from evidence rather than ideology.

Public ownership of national resources — water, energy, oil — is associated with the left. It is also simply *reasonable*: these are the nation's assets and they should serve the nation first.

Strong national defence, controlled borders, and support for British business are associated with the right. They are also simply *reasonable*: a country must be secure and capable.

Universal basic services — free school meals, public facilities, healthcare, UBI — are associated with the left. They are also simply *good economics*: a population that is fed, housed, and healthy costs far less in crisis management than one that isn't.

We take what works. We leave what doesn't. We show our working.

**Evidence, not tribe. Always.**

# THE LEADER — ADDRESSED DIRECTLY

Britain Forward requires ministers to have relevant expertise. Critics ask: does the party leader?

Yes — because leading a party and a country requires a different expertise from running a department.

The Prime Minister needs: the ability to build and hold a coalition, to communicate complex ideas plainly, to make decisions under pressure, to hold a long-term vision against short-term noise, and to be publicly accountable for outcomes.

These skills are found in people who have *led large organisations through genuine change* — not necessarily in Parliament.

Our leader has not come from left or right. They have come from the country.

**What we require:**
1. Demonstrable record of delivering complex change at scale
2. No prior major party affiliation — genuinely new
3. Origin outside the London political establishment
4. Ability to speak plainly to ordinary people without condescension
5. Willingness to be held publicly accountable — including by their own party, under the Vote of Incompetence mechanism

*The specific individual is determined by member selection. This defines what we seek.*

And if they fail — we remove them. That is the real answer to the leadership question. Not "trust us." But: "We have built a mechanism to hold them accountable."

# PILLAR ONE: FIX THE SYSTEM
## Democratic & Political Renewal

*Before we fix anything, we fix how decisions get made.*

---

## 1.1 MINISTERIAL COMPETENCE

Ministers will have demonstrable expertise or professional experience relevant to their department.

A Health Secretary will have worked in healthcare. A Chancellor will have worked in economics. An Education Secretary will have worked in education.

This is not elitism. A surgeon performs your operation. The people running Britain's critical systems must understand those systems. No more learning on the job at the nation's expense.

---

## 1.2 ELECTORAL REFORM

**Ranked Weighted Voting replaces First Past the Post.**

Voters rank candidates in preference order. Points awarded by ranking — rewarding broad appeal, not just intense minority support. Votes in every constituency count. Tactical voting becomes unnecessary. Cooperation is incentivised.

**Compulsory Voting — The Australian Model.**

Every eligible citizen votes. Spoiled ballots and None of the Above are valid — but invisible abstention is not.

Why: a government elected by 35% of those who bothered to vote does not represent Britain. Under compulsory voting, every candidate must earn your participation — even if your choice is "none of you."

NOTA votes are counted, published, and taken seriously as a formal signal of public dissatisfaction.

**Coalition Red Lines — Stated In Advance:**

Britain Forward will not enter coalition with any party that:
- Opposes electoral reform — it is the foundation
- Seeks to reverse public ownership of core infrastructure
- Abandons the ministerial competence requirement

Everything else is negotiable. These three are not.

## 1.3 CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK

**The Monarchy — Ultimate Seat of Sovereign Authority**

The Monarch remains as the ultimate constitutional backstop — the final sovereign authority in genuine constitutional crisis. Largely ceremonial. Largely silent. But present and real.

No single political actor can capture the entire constitutional apparatus while the Monarchy exists as a non-political anchor. They reign. They do not govern.

**The House of Lords — Reform, Not Automatic Abolition**

The Lords in its current form is indefensible: unelected, unaccountable, stuffed with political appointees and hereditary privilege.

But its *function* — scrutiny, revision, the long view — is valuable and must be preserved.

Options under public consultation:
- Full replacement with an elected Senate with fixed terms and regional representation
- Hybrid chamber: partially elected, partially expert-appointed through a fully independent process
- Full abolition with functions transferred to a strengthened National Oversight Council

*This will be subject to public consultation and a binding referendum. Britain Forward does not impose constitutional settlements. We propose, consult, and let Britain decide.*

**National Oversight Council**

Established as a scrutiny body alongside Lords reform:
- Mixed composition: elected members plus independently appointed experts — judiciary, science, ethics, civic society
- Selection process: fully independent, no government appointments
- Power to delay and refer legislation for review — not to block permanently
- Integrated with judicial review

**Parliament — London For Now, Region-First Direction**

Parliament stays in London during this Parliament. However:
- Government departments begin phased relocation to regional cities immediately
- A formal independent commission established to assess and plan Parliament's future location — reporting within five years
- Regional parliamentary offices opened in major cities — genuine presence, not symbolic

The direction is set. The timeline is honest.

## 1.4 POLITICAL ACCOUNTABILITY — CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

**The rule is simple: MPs do not personally benefit from policies they shape.**

**The Conflict of Interest Standard:**
- MPs must not have been involved with any company significantly affected by their ministerial decisions in the previous **5 years** before taking office
- MPs must not be involved with any such company **while serving**
- MPs must not join any such company for **10 years after** leaving office

**Where benefit demonstrably flows to an MP through family connection**, the same restrictions apply. We acknowledge this is legally complex and potentially unfair in some cases — the test is *demonstrable benefit flowing to the MP*, not mere proximity.

This is designed to prevent, specifically and explicitly, the kind of arrangement where a minister's household or immediate associates benefit financially from policies that minister has shaped. Britain's public life has suffered too many such cases. They end under Britain Forward.

**MP Pay — Public Service, Not Career Enrichment**

Being an MP is a public service and a civic duty. It should attract people who want to serve, not people who want to profit.

- MPs are paid the **median wage of their constituency** — they live as their constituents live
- Housing: genuine travel and accommodation costs covered for MPs representing constituencies outside London; this covers a room or flat, not a second property purchase
- **Zero outside income** while serving — an MP's full attention belongs to their constituents
- Expenses: fully transparent, publicly searchable, audited quarterly; personal and family expenses are never eligible; the standard is "would a reasonable constituent accept this as a work cost?"
- Pay rises: subject to the same process and constraints as public sector pay — not self-determined

## 2.1 THE SOVEREIGN WEALTH FUND — HONESTLY FUNDED

**Where the money comes from:**

**1. Nationalised asset revenues**
Water, energy, and oil brought under public ownership generate returns currently flowing to private shareholders and foreign funds. Those returns go to the British Sovereign Wealth Fund instead.

**2. Stopping mismanagement**
A significant portion of the fund comes from *not wasting money*:
- Ending unjustifiable MP expenses and political self-enrichment
- Ending procurement corruption and donor-linked contracts
- Reforming welfare administration that costs more to administer than it saves
- Pharmaceutical price negotiation — we overpay for drugs developed partly with British public research funding
- Whole-life infrastructure costing — building once, properly, rather than patching repeatedly
- Reducing NHS crisis costs through prevention — cheaper to keep people well than treat them when seriously ill

**3. British oil and gas**
Domestic production for domestic use first. Surplus sold internationally at market rate. Revenue to the fund.

**The honest position on scale:**
We will not promise Norway's fund in five years. Britain's North Sea is past peak production. We are promising Norway's *model* — starting now, building over decades. Every year we fail to start is a year of compounded loss.

The fund invests in British infrastructure first. Surplus invested globally. Returns reinvested in public services and future generations.

---

## 2.2 NATIONALISATION — THE THREE-TIER MODEL

Britain Forward proposes public ownership of core national infrastructure. This is not the 1970s. It is the Norwegian, French, and German model, brought to Britain.

**Tier One — Infrastructure Layer: 100% Publicly Owned**

The physical networks — pipes, wires, cables, rails, the actual infrastructure — are owned outright by the British public, managed by public bodies:

- **British Water** — owns and maintains the water network
- **British Grid** — owns and maintains energy transmission and distribution
- **British Rail Infrastructure** — owns the track, stations, and signalling

*Direct accountability: when water is contaminated, British Water is accountable. Not a private company, then an in-company review, then an independent review, then a regulator, then a court. One body. Direct accountability. Real consequences.*

**Tier Two — Operating Layer: Majority Public, Citizen Shares**

Companies operating *through* the public infrastructure:
- Government holds majority ownership (51-75%)
- Remaining shares available to **British citizens directly** — not institutional investors, not foreign funds
- Citizens receive annual dividends — small but real; a genuine stake in national assets
- Operating companies retain some market discipline while profit primarily serves the public

**Tier Three — Competitive Layer: Private and Free**

Services and innovations built *on top of* the public infrastructure compete freely. The network is public. What runs on it can compete. Innovation is encouraged at this level.

**The result:**
- Clear accountability for essential services — straight to the top, no chain of reviews
- Profit from national assets goes to British people, not foreign shareholders
- Citizens own a piece of their country
- Competitive pressure maintained where it's actually useful

## 2.3 UNIVERSAL BASIC INCOME — THE FULL FRAMEWORK

UBI is not a radical experiment. It is a rational simplification of a benefits system that currently costs nearly as much to administer as it pays out.

**The Rate:** Approximately £100 per week (to be confirmed by evidence review and economic modelling before implementation)

**Who receives it:**

| Group | UBI | Additional income |
|---|---|---|
| Working people | ✅ Full UBI | + Their earned wage |
| Retired people | ✅ Full UBI | + Their earned pension |
| Job-seeking unemployed | ✅ Full UBI | + Active employment support |
| Non-seeking, non-disabled unemployed | ✅ Full UBI | Nothing additional |
| Disabled people | ✅ Full UBI | + Disability support (separate, needs-based) |
| Students | ✅ Full UBI | Replaces most student maintenance |
| Criminals (under sentence) | ❌ No UBI | Cannot reclaim for period under sentence |
| Non-integrated non-natives (under 5 years) | ❌ No UBI | Until 5-year integration threshold met |
| Foreign nationals | ❌ No UBI | — |

**On the "laziness" objection:**

The evidence from UBI pilots worldwide — Finland, Kenya, Canada, Alaska — consistently shows that UBI *increases* workforce participation, not reduces it.

Why: people in jobs they *chose* and can *afford to take* stay in those jobs longer, perform better, and burn out less. When the choice isn't "take any job that pays enough to survive" but "find work that suits me," the result is a better-matched, more productive workforce.

Yes — some people will live on £100 a week and not work. They will have a safe, austere, very quiet life without luxuries. This is their choice to make. And it costs the state less than the current system of means-testing, sanctions, appeals, and tribunals.

**On higher earners:**

UBI is taxed back from higher earners through the income tax system. Those earning above a defined threshold effectively return their UBI through marginal tax rates. The net cost of UBI is substantially lower than the gross figure.

**On replacing existing benefits:**

UBI replaces the administrative complexity of most means-tested benefits. Some targeted support remains — disability, childcare, housing in high-cost areas. But the vast majority of the current system is replaced by one simple, universal payment.

**Implementation:** Piloted properly — real pilots, rigorous evaluation — before national rollout. This Parliament establishes the pilots. The next Parliament makes the rollout decision based on evidence.

# PILLAR THREE: NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE
## Roads, Cities, Energy & Connectivity

*Build it properly. Once. Right.*

---

## 3.1 ROADS — REBUILT TO LAST

British roads are resurfaced constantly because cheap materials degrade quickly. We pay again and again for the same roads. This ends.

- Long-life composite surfaces — built to last
- Whole-life costing in all road contracts — cheapest upfront is not cheapest overall
- Road-embedded EV charging — inductive coils in key surfaces; vehicles charge while moving; solves the commercial payload problem (a laden electric van currently manages ~50 miles; continuous road charging removes that constraint)
- Road hierarchies redesigned for modern freight, cycling, and autonomous vehicles
- Green corridors alongside all new roads — wildlife routes integrated
- Usage-based road pricing replaces fuel duty as EVs grow — fair, hypothecated to roads and transport

---

## 3.2 PUBLIC DIGNITY INFRASTRUCTURE

*The basics. Working. For everyone.*

**Public Toilets**
- Statutory minimum provision — councils cannot cut below the floor
- Ring-fenced central funding
- All A-road laybys and rest stops: free, maintained, lit
- HGV rest areas: proper facilities, showers, safe — truckers are legally required to rest; the infrastructure must exist
- Petrol stations on public roads: free access required for any public contract
- Changing Places facilities in all new major public buildings
- Free at point of use — 30p turnstiles abolished
- National cleanliness, lighting, and safety standards — inspected and enforced

**Public Showers**
- Available at community centres, leisure facilities, and dedicated hubs
- Serves homeless people, outdoor workers, cyclists — infrastructure, not charity
- Linked to public swimming pools and leisure centres where possible

**Public Kitchens**
- Accessible cooking spaces at community centres, libraries, and dedicated facilities
- Open any time — equipped, clean, safe
- Evening cooking classes and skills workshops run from the same spaces
- Available to: people without home cooking facilities, those facing energy cost pressure, people in temporary accommodation, hotel guests, anyone who wants to learn
- Not a soup kitchen. A kitchen. Open to everyone.

**Public Orchards & Community Growing**
- Public orchards planted in parks and green spaces — fruit freely available
- Three categories clearly marked:
  - *Public Orchard*: open harvest, anyone takes freely
  - *Commons Reserve*: protected seed and mother trees regenerating the public orchards; not for harvest
  - *Private*: clearly marked, not public access
- Community growing spaces — allotments, raised beds, shared gardens alongside
- Connected to school growing programmes

## 3.3 ENERGY — BRITAIN POWERS BRITAIN

**The goal: Britain does not depend on foreign energy.**

**Renewables:**
- Solar: rooftops, not fields. Mandatory on all new builds. Retrofit programme prioritising social housing.
- Wind: onshore and offshore expansion
- Tidal and wave: world-class British tidal resources — currently strategic negligence; exploitation begins
- Hydro: Scotland's river network supports community micro-hydro across dozens of sites — each local scheme frees national grid capacity
- Geothermal: Cornwall's deep geothermal project as expansion template

**Nuclear:** Stable baseload. Investment in next-generation reactors.

**Oil and Gas:** British production maintained during transition. British oil for British people first. Surplus sold internationally. Revenue to Sovereign Wealth Fund.

**Smart Grid:** Upgraded for distributed rooftop generation. Excess household power bought back fairly — energy bills become energy income for many homes.

**District Heating:** Shared heat networks in new cities — efficient, lower carbon.

## 3.4 THE HIGH-SPEED RAIL PROGRAMME

Japan builds high-speed rail through earthquake zones on mountainous islands — on time and on budget. Britain is flat, geologically stable, and comparatively small.

HS2 failed because it was mismanaged, over-scoped, and subjected to political interference at every stage. That is a lesson in how *not* to do it. It is not an argument against doing it.

**Britain Forward commits:**
- A properly scoped, engineering-led high-speed rail programme
- Engineering decisions made by engineers — politics removed from procurement and design
- Transparent budget with genuine contingency
- A national high-speed network as a long-term direction — routes prioritised by economic benefit, not political geography

*This is a direction and a commitment to doing it properly. The timescale is honest: this is a multi-Parliament project. It will not be cancelled when it becomes inconvenient.*

## 3.5 CITIES — BUILDING FOR THE FUTURE

**New Planned Cities**

New cities, built right from the start:
- Located outside the South East — redistributing economic opportunity
- Mixed housing: owned, social, cooperative
- Employment planned alongside residential — not dormitory towns
- Green corridors built in from day one

**Multi-Layer Urban Design — Long-Term Vision**

The most liveable cities are designed for people, not cars. For new cities and major new districts, the design standard moves toward:

- Underground or separated heavy transit and vehicle routes
- Human-scale surfaces: walking, cycling, green space, clean air
- Rooftop solar and green roofs standard

This is acknowledged as ambitious for existing cities. It is the design standard for new development, applied progressively over decades. It is where we are going, not a promise for this Parliament.

**Sky bridges** in commercial and civic districts — covered pedestrian connections between buildings, reducing surface congestion.

**Listed and Derelict Buildings**
- Listed building reform: tiered review; repair pathways that work; demolition permitted for genuinely unsalvageable structures with replacement requirements
- Derelict buildings: compulsory purchase at fair value; rebuild or convert to greenspace; long-term dereliction is not tolerated

## 4.8 POLICIES UNDER EVIDENCE REVIEW

Britain Forward is evidence-led. The following will be legislated *when the evidence and legal frameworks are sufficiently robust* — not before, and not never.

**Drug Policy Reform**
Current classification is widely criticised as inconsistent and ineffective. The evidence from Portugal, Canada, and the Netherlands shows that treating drug use as a health issue reduces harm, that regulation enables victim identification and protection, and that criminalising users prevents them seeking help.

*Key principle:* Curtailing drug misuse is easier when drugs are legal — you can regulate, tax, target enforcement on trafficking and exploitation, and identify and protect victims rather than criminalising them alongside their abusers.

Evidence review underway. Harm-based regulatory framework will follow when the evidence supports it.

**Sex Work Policy**
Legal, regulated sex work enables worker protections, health standards, and — crucially — identification and protection of trafficking victims, which is far easier in a regulated environment than a criminalised one.

Evidence review of international models (New Zealand, Germany, Netherlands) underway.

**Relationship Law Reform**
Legal recognition of diverse adult relationship structures — with full safeguards, independent legal advice requirements, and clear frameworks for finances, parental responsibility, and dissolution — is under active legal review.

**Personal Liberty and Decriminalisation**
Britain Forward believes that what consenting adults do in ways that harm nobody else is not the state's business. The practical and legal frameworks for specific liberalisations are under review.

*These are not hidden policies. The direction is stated. The timing depends on evidence, not electoral calculation.*

## 6.1 DEFENCE — STRONG, SOVEREIGN, NOT FOR HIRE

Britain will be strong. Britain will be prepared. Britain will not be used as a tool of other nations' agendas.

- Strong, modern, capable military — for genuine defence and credible deterrence
- Cyber defence: core national infrastructure
- Domestic defence industry capability — not dependent on foreign supply for critical equipment
- Veterans properly supported

**Britain WILL:**
- Defend itself and treaty allies against genuine military aggression
- Honour genuine defensive alliance obligations
- Maintain credible deterrence

**Britain WILL NOT:**
- Be bullied into offensive military operations that don't serve Britain's genuine security interests
- Commit troops or weapons without full parliamentary debate and public accountability
- Deploy offensive capability in conflicts where Britain has no direct security interest

The distinction: defending against genuine aggression — yes, always. Adventures in other nations' conflicts on behalf of allies — only with parliamentary mandate and genuine British interest.

# THE LONG GAME — WHERE BRITAIN IS HEADING

This is not a party for the next election. It is a movement for the long term.

The 22nd century is the target. The policies in this platform are the first steps.

By the time this work is complete — over decades, not Parliaments — Britain should be:

**Energy sovereign** — producing more than it needs, selling surplus, funding future generations.

**A country that works** — roads that last, cities designed for people, trains that connect, water that doesn't leak, broadband that reaches everywhere.

**A capable people** — equipped to think critically, to work well, to participate in democracy meaningfully.

**A fair country** — where effort leads to reward, the basics are provided, and the system serves people rather than extracting from them.

**A world leader** — not through military dominance but through excellence in medicine, technology, clean energy, food, and engineering.

**A sovereign democracy** — making its own decisions, for its own people, answerable to nobody but Britain.

We are building toward the country Britain should always have been.

Starting now.

**Not Left. Not Right. Forward.**
