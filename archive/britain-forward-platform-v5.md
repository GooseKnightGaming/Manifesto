## 1.2 ELECTORAL REFORM

*(Full detail in sub-document: Voting System Overhaul)*

**Ranked Weighted Voting replaces First Past the Post.**

Voters rank candidates in preference order. Points awarded by ranking. Winners must command broad support — not just an intense minority. No more wasted votes. No more tactical voting. Every constituency matters.

**Compulsory Participation.**

Every eligible citizen participates. Spoiled ballots and NOTA are valid. Invisible abstention replaced by formal registered dissatisfaction.

*HRA compatibility assessment published before legislation is introduced. Incentive-based alternative prepared if compulsion is found unlawful.*

**None of the Above on every ballot.**

Formally counted and published. High NOTA results trigger mandatory constituency accountability. NOTA winning outright triggers fresh election within 60 days — original candidates ineligible.

**Coalition Red Lines — stated before any election:**

Britain Forward will not coalition with any party that:
- Opposes ranked weighted voting
- Reverses public ownership of core infrastructure
- Abandons ministerial competence requirements

Everything else is negotiable. These three are not.

## 1.3 CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK

**The Monarchy — Constitutional Custodian of Last Resort**

The Monarch is not the sovereign. Sovereignty resides in the British people. The Monarch is a constitutional custodian — a non-political safeguard ensuring no actor can capture the entire constitutional apparatus. In genuine crisis only, acting to restore the conditions for popular sovereignty. Ceremonial in normal times. Present and real in extremis.

**The House of Lords — Public Decision**

Current form: indefensible. Function: valuable.

Britain Forward publishes three reform options, consults publicly, holds a binding referendum. The public decides. Not us.

Options: elected Senate; hybrid chamber (partially elected, partially independently expert-appointed); full abolition with functions to National Oversight Council.

**National Oversight Council**

- Composition: elected members plus independently appointed experts — no government appointments, ever
- Selection: fully independent, publicly transparent process
- Powers: delay and refer legislation — not permanently block
- Integrated with judicial review

**Parliament — London Now, Direction Set**

Parliament remains in London this Parliament. Government departments: phased regional relocation begins immediately. Independent commission assesses Parliament's future location — reports within five years. Public referendum decides.

## 1.4 POLITICAL ACCOUNTABILITY

**Conflict of Interest — The Precise Standard**

*The distinction that matters:* sector expertise is encouraged. Personal financial benefit from specific decisions is prohibited.

*The test:* Did this company materially benefit from a specific decision this MP made or influenced?

- No involvement with such companies in the previous **5 years** before taking office
- No involvement while **serving**
- No involvement for **10 years after** leaving office
- Where demonstrable benefit flows to an MP *through* a family member: same standard applies to that benefit

This is designed specifically to prevent the kind of arrangement where a minister's household financially benefits from policies that minister has shaped.

**MP Pay — Public Service Rate**

- Paid the national median wage with London cost-of-living adjustment only
- Zero outside income while serving
- Genuine work accommodation expenses only — not second property purchase
- All expenses: publicly searchable, audited quarterly, personal costs never eligible
- Pay rises: same process as public sector — not self-determined

## 2.3 UNIVERSAL BASIC INCOME

*(Full detail in sub-document: UBI & 4-Day Week)*

**Target rate:** ~£100/week. Confirmed by pilot evidence and independent modelling before national rollout.

**Who qualifies:** British citizens and settled residents (5+ years). High-skilled visa holders after 2 years. Standard visa holders after 5 years.

UBI suspended during criminal sentence — resumes on day of release. Active resettlement package on release.

**Net cost:** Independent modelling required. Gross figure (£260bn) is not the relevant number — tax recoupture from higher earners and benefits system replacement savings substantially reduce it.

**The lazy objection:** Evidence from Finland, Kenya, Canada, Alaska consistently shows UBI increases workforce participation. People in jobs they chose stay longer and perform better.

**Implementation:** Pilots this Parliament. National rollout decision next Parliament, based on evidence.

## 5.2 THE EDUCATION-TO-EMPLOYMENT PATHWAY

*A new, structured transition from training to work.*

One of the most persistent failures of British education is the gap between qualification and employment. People train for years and then face a cliff edge: no experience, no network, no clear route in.

Britain Forward will close this gap through **Formal Education-Employment Liaisons** — structured, mandatory partnerships between educational institutions and relevant employers.

### How It Works

Every school, college, university, and apprenticeship programme establishes formal liaison relationships with relevant employers and institutions in their primary subject areas.

These are not tokenistic careers fairs. They are structured pathways:

**Teacher training:**
Trainee teachers are placed in partner schools from year one. Upon qualification, they have priority consideration for permanent positions in those schools. The school knows them. They know the school.

**Medical and nursing:**
NHS trusts and GP practices as formal partners throughout training — placements, mentoring, and direct employment pathways for graduates.

**Engineering and technical:**
Industry partners co-design curriculum elements and provide guaranteed interview pathways for graduates meeting defined standards.

**Archaeology, history, museum studies:**
Formal partnerships between universities and museums, heritage organisations, local authorities — internships during study, direct pipeline into relevant roles.

**Legal:**
Law firms, barristers' chambers, local authorities, and legal aid organisations as formal partners — placements during training, structured pupillage and training contract pipelines.

**Creative industries:**
Arts schools partner with theatres, studios, galleries, publishers, game studios — placements during study, showcasing to relevant employers.

**Trades and vocational:**
Apprenticeship programmes are *already* employment-linked in principle — the liaison model formalises and strengthens this, ensuring apprenticeships lead to genuine employment in the trained trade.

### The Requirements

**For educational institutions:**
- Minimum number of formal liaison agreements per department, relevant to that department's subject
- Reported annually to a new Office for Educational Transition
- Institutions with poor transition-to-employment rates subject to review

**For employers (in publicly funded sectors):**
- Public sector employers (NHS, schools, councils, government bodies): required to participate in liaison schemes
- Private employers receiving public funding or contracts: required to participate

**For employers (private sector generally):**
- Tax incentives for participation — qualifying liaison employers receive employment tax relief for graduates hired through the scheme
- Recognition scheme — "Britain Forward Employer Partner" designation for organisations with strong transition records

**For students:**
- Liaison placements are part of the course — not optional extras
- Portfolio and work record built during study becomes part of the qualification record
- Not an internship mill — placements are paid where work is performed, or structured as genuine learning within the qualification

### What This Fixes

**The experience paradox:** Entry-level jobs requiring experience become structurally impossible when training doesn't include experience. The liaison model means graduates have relevant experience before they graduate.

**The network gap:** Professional networks — who you know — determine employment outcomes more than qualifications in many fields. Students who enter training without professional networks graduate without them. The liaison model builds professional relationships during training.

**The geography problem:** Students who study in one city and want to work in another often have no local network. Regional liaison agreements create pathways in the regions where students actually want to live and work.

**The subject relevance question:** Graduates of archaeology degrees becoming baristas is a waste of training investment and a human disappointment. Formal pathways into relevant work — museums, heritage, local government, consultancy — mean the investment pays off for the person and the country.

# FISCAL FRAMEWORK — THE HONEST VERSION

*(Full detail in sub-document: Money)*

**Revenue spending** (NHS, UBI, welfare, education, public services): funded from tax revenues and nationalised asset revenues.

**Capital investment** (roads, rail, cities, water, energy): financed through long-term public bonds, repaid by the assets' own revenues over their operational lifetime.

*These are not the same thing. Treating infrastructure investment as current spending is how governments make bad decisions.*

**How we pay for it:**
1. Nationalised asset revenues — profits currently flowing abroad stay in Britain
2. Pharmaceutical savings — we stop overpaying for drugs
3. Stopped mismanagement — procurement reform, benefits administration savings
4. Road pricing — replaces fuel duty fairly
5. UBI tax recoupture — higher earners return their UBI through income tax
6. Long-term economic growth — from investment in people and infrastructure

Full independent fiscal assessment commissioned and published before any general election. We show our working.

# THE LONG GAME

This platform is not for the next election. It is a direction for the long term.

Every decision in this platform is evaluated against a simple question: *Does this make Britain better in twenty, forty, a hundred years?*

Roads built to last. Cities designed for people. Energy from Britain, for Britain. A population equipped to think, work, and participate. A state that is accountable and delivers. A country where the returns from national resources go to the nation.

This is where we are heading.

Starting now.
