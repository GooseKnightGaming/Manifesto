# WHO WE ARE

Britain Forward is a movement for national renewal.

Not a left-wing party. Not a right-wing party. Something different — a movement that starts from a simple observation:

**Britain is not broken. But its systems are.**

The NHS is full of brilliant people being let down by broken management. The roads are full of potholes because we keep paying to patch them instead of paying once to fix them. The housing crisis exists not because Britain lacks land but because decisions were never made to use it properly. Politicians make decisions about industries they've never worked in. People do everything right and still can't get ahead.

These are not problems of left or right. They are problems of a system that has stopped working for the people it exists to serve.

Britain Forward exists to fix the system. To build something that actually works. To plan properly for the future instead of reacting endlessly to the present.

**That is what Forward means.**

---

# CORE PRINCIPLES

These apply to every policy. If a policy contradicts them, the policy is wrong.

**National Capability** — Britain must be able to provide for itself in critical areas. Energy, food, medicine, defence, infrastructure.

**Fairness & Merit** — Opportunity based on ability, effort, and contribution — not background, connections, or luck.

**Public Strength** — Essential systems must serve the people, not extract from them.

**Pragmatism** — Evidence-based policy over ideology. What works, not what sounds good.

**Future Focus** — Long-term thinking over short-term gain. We will build things that last.

**Equal Responsibility** — The same rules apply to everyone. No exceptions for wealth, status, or connections.

# PILLAR ONE: FIX THE SYSTEM
## Democratic & Political Renewal

*This is our primary purpose. Everything else depends on it.*

The reason people don't trust any party isn't fundamentally about policy. It's that they've stopped believing the system can deliver anything. Every manifesto promise feels hollow because the machinery that's supposed to implement it is broken, captured, or staffed by people who've never done the job before.

Before we can fix anything else, we fix how this country makes decisions.

---

## 1.1 MINISTERIAL COMPETENCE

**The single most important reform.**

Ministers will have demonstrable expertise or professional experience relevant to their department.

A Health Secretary will have worked in healthcare. An Education Secretary will have worked in education. A Chancellor will have worked in economics or finance.

This is not elitism. A plumber fixes your pipes. A surgeon performs your operation. The people running Britain's critical systems should understand those systems.

No more career politicians parachuted into departments they've never encountered. No more learning on the job at the nation's expense.

---

## 1.2 ELECTORAL REFORM

Replace First Past the Post with a Ranked Weighted Voting system.

- Voters rank candidates in order of preference
- Points awarded by ranking — rewarding candidates with broad support, not just passionate minorities
- Reduces extreme polarisation; encourages cooperation
- Makes votes in every constituency count, not just marginal seats
- None of the Above option included on every ballot
- Coalitions formed where no single party achieves majority — compromise built into the system

---

## 1.3 CONSTITUTIONAL REFORM

**Abolish the House of Lords**

An unelected second chamber with legislative power is indefensible in a modern democracy. It goes.

**Keep the Monarchy — as a limited constitutional backstop only**

Not as a political actor. As a last-resort constitutional safeguard — available in genuine constitutional crisis, silent otherwise. This maintains stability while removing undemocratic privilege.

**Introduce a National Oversight Council**

A new body replacing the Lords' scrutiny function, properly designed:
- Mixed composition: elected members, expert appointments (judiciary, science, ethics, civic society)
- Power to delay and refer legislation for review — not to block permanently
- Independent of government, accountable to the public
- Integrated with judicial review

**Three-layer constitutional stability:**
Parliament (democratic) → National Oversight Council (scrutiny) → Monarchy (emergency backstop)

This is more robust than the current system, not less.

---

## 1.4 POLITICAL REFORM

- Lobbying fully registered and transparent — who is lobbying whom, for what, and at what cost
- Political donations capped and fully disclosed
- MPs' second jobs limited and disclosed — public service is a full-time commitment
- Revolving door between government and industry regulated — minimum cooling-off period before taking related private sector roles
- None of the Above votes counted and published — a standing measure of public confidence

---

## 1.5 CIVIL SERVICE REFORM

- Performance-based review — civil servants accountable for outcomes, not just processes
- Digital-first government — services designed for the 21st century
- Cross-department integration — end the silo culture that produces contradictory policies
- Political neutrality protected — civil servants serve the public, not the party in power

---

## 1.6 TRANSPARENT TAX SPENDING

Every taxpayer contributes. Every taxpayer deserves to know where the money goes.

- Annual plain-English spending breakdown published — national and local
- Available in every library, GP surgery, job centre, and online
- Independently verified
- Broken down by category with actual proportions

This is cheap to implement and transforms democratic accountability.

# PILLAR TWO: BUILD THE ECONOMY
## National Capability & Sovereignty

---

## 2.1 SOVEREIGN WEALTH FUND

Establish a British Sovereign Wealth Fund — seeded by:
- Nationalised infrastructure revenues
- Windfall taxes on excess profits
- Strategic industry dividends
- Long-term public bond issuance

Managed independently. Invested in Britain first. Surplus invested globally. Returns reinvested in public services and infrastructure.

Norway did this with oil. France did this with energy. Britain had both resources and gave the revenue away. We will not repeat that mistake.

---

## 2.2 PUBLIC OWNERSHIP OF CORE INFRASTRUCTURE

Public ownership or control of:
- Energy networks
- Rail infrastructure
- Water
- Key utilities
- Telecommunications backbone
- Steel production

**Model:** Public ownership of core systems — the networks, the pipes, the wires — with competitive private and cooperative layers operating services within them. Not Soviet-style state operation. A modern, efficient hybrid that serves the public rather than extracting from them.

---

## 2.3 BRITISH OIL — FOR THE BRITISH PEOPLE

Britain's North Sea oil and gas resources should primarily serve Britain.

- Bring British oil fields under national ownership or significant public stake
- Domestic energy needs met first
- Surplus sold internationally at market rate — revenue to Sovereign Wealth Fund
- Norway's Equinor model provides the template

The resource belongs to the nation. The profit should too.

---

## 2.4 INVESTMENT IN BRITISH INDUSTRY

- British manufacturing supported and expanded — tax incentives, procurement priority, skills investment
- Technology and innovation sector backed — R&D tax relief, public investment in emerging sectors
- Regional economic development — investment distributed deliberately, not concentrated in London
- British businesses supported before surplus exported

---

## 2.5 CORPORATE STANDARDS

- Pay ratio limits — executive pay cannot exceed a defined multiple of the lowest-paid employee in the same organisation
- Right to Repair legislation — manufacturers must provide parts and repair information; reduces waste, saves consumers money, creates repair industry jobs
- Zero-hours contract reform — guaranteed minimum hours for regularly employed workers
- Charity reform — funds must demonstrably reach intended causes; executive pay within charities regulated

---

## 2.6 BANKING & FINANCE

- Support for local and community banks — alternatives to the major retail banks
- Crackdown on exploitative lending — payday loan regulation, rent-to-own reform
- Long-term investment incentivised over short-term speculation
- Pharmaceutical price negotiation — a national body with real bargaining power negotiates NHS drug costs; we currently pay far more than we should

# PILLAR THREE: NATIONAL INFRASTRUCTURE
## Roads, Cities, Energy & Connectivity

*"We're not patching what's broken. We're building what should have been built."*

---

## 3.1 ROADS — REBUILT PROPERLY

British roads are resurfaced constantly because cheap materials degrade quickly. We pay again and again for the same roads. This ends.

**Materials:** Long-life composite surfaces — weather-resistant, permeable where needed, built to last. Whole-life costing replaces upfront-cost thinking in all road contracts.

**Road-Embedded EV Charging:** Inductive charging coils embedded in key road surfaces during rebuild. Vehicles charge while moving — solving the commercial EV payload problem (a laden electric van currently manages ~50 miles; road charging removes that limitation entirely). Replaces fuel duty revenue as EVs grow.

**Design:** Road hierarchies redesigned for modern freight, cycling, and autonomous vehicles. Green corridors alongside new roads — wildlife routes integrated into engineering design.

**Rest Stops & Laybys:** Every layby and rest stop on A-roads and above upgraded with free public toilet facilities, EV charging, and basic amenities. HGV rest areas upgraded substantially — showers, proper facilities, safe lighting. Truckers are legally required to rest. The facilities to do so must exist.

**Petrol stations** on public roads and holding public contracts: free toilet access required. No more paying 30p to use a toilet on a public road.

---

## 3.2 PUBLIC TOILETS — THE DIGNITY INFRASTRUCTURE

A country that works properly provides the basics.

- Statutory minimum public toilet provision — councils cannot cut below a defined floor
- Ring-fenced funding from central government
- Town centres, parks, transport hubs, rural visitor sites — all covered
- Changing Places facilities (for people with complex disabilities) required in all new major public buildings
- Free at point of use — the 30p turnstile is abolished
- National standards for cleanliness, lighting, safety, and accessibility — inspected and enforced
- Community Toilet Scheme nationalised — businesses receive payment for opening facilities to the public

---

## 3.3 BULLET TRAIN

If Japan can build high-speed rail through earthquake zones on mountainous islands, Britain — flat, geologically stable, and comparatively small — has no credible engineering excuse.

- London to Edinburgh: under 2 hours
- London to Manchester: under 45 minutes
- Transforms regional connectivity, distributes economic activity, reduces domestic flights
- Designed and built to the standard that actually works — not the compromised, delayed, cancelled version

---

## 3.4 ELECTRIC VEHICLES

- Road-embedded charging solves the commercial range problem (see 3.1)
- National EV infrastructure fund for fleet operators transitioning from diesel
- Charge point rollout at industrial estates, depots, logistics hubs, and rural areas — no postcode lottery
- R&D investment in solid-state batteries and rapid charging — Britain leads the science

---

## 3.5 ENERGY — INDEPENDENCE AND SECURITY

**Dual-track approach:** Security now, transformation over time.

**Renewables:**
- Solar: on roofs, not in farmer's fields. Fields are for food. Roofs are for power.
- Mandatory rooftop solar on all new residential and commercial builds
- Retrofit programme for existing housing — social housing and low-income households first
- Wind: onshore and offshore expansion
- **Tidal and wave energy** — Britain has some of the world's best tidal resources and is barely using them. This is a competitive national advantage being ignored.
- **Hydro:** Scotland's river network makes micro and community hydro schemes viable across dozens of sites. Each scheme powering one town frees national grid capacity. Aggregate effect is significant.
- **Geothermal:** Cornwall's deep geothermal project provides the template for expansion

**Nuclear:** Modern nuclear provides stable baseload alongside variable renewables. Investment in next-generation reactors.

**Oil and gas:** Domestic production for domestic security during transition — not abandoned prematurely. British oil for British people first (see 2.3).

**Smart Grid:** Upgraded national grid to handle distributed rooftop generation. Excess household power bought back at fair rates — energy bills become energy income for many homes.

**District Heating:** Networks in new cities and major urban areas — efficient, shared, lower carbon than individual boiler systems.

---

## 3.6 NEW PLANNED CITIES

The housing crisis is not just about number of homes. It's about concentration — all demand in places already at capacity.

New planned cities, built right from the start:
- Located deliberately in areas of lower economic activity — redistributing opportunity
- Multi-layer urban design built in from day one (see 3.7)
- Mixed housing tenure: owned, social, cooperative
- Employment districts planned alongside residential — not dormitory towns
- Green corridors and open space integrated — not squeezed in later
- Public transport from day one — not added as afterthought

Precedents: Milton Keynes, Stevenage, South Korea's Sejong City, Australia's Canberra.

---

## 3.7 MULTI-LAYER CITIES

The most efficient, liveable cities design for people — not for cars.

**Underground level:** Heavy transit (metro, tram, rail) and private vehicles — fast, separated, weather-proof. Underground freight delivery for city centres — lorries never surface in residential areas.

**Surface level:** Walking and cycling only. Green space, trees, clean air. Outdoor dining, markets, community space. Emergency vehicles excepted.

**Rooftops:** Solar panels standard. Green roofs where solar isn't installed — insulation, biodiversity, rainfall management. Community gardens where feasible.

**Sky bridges** connecting buildings in commercial and civic districts — reducing ground congestion, adding character, enabling covered pedestrian movement.

Result: Congestion gone from city surfaces. Air quality transformed. Cities become genuinely pleasant to live in.

---

## 3.8 REGIONAL EQUALITY — NO MORE LONDON PRIVILEGE

London's infrastructure spending per head has historically far exceeded every other region. This is a policy choice. It will be reversed.

- Per capita infrastructure investment legally equalised — written into statute, independently monitored
- Government departments physically relocated out of London — civil service jobs distributed nationally
- Regional economic hubs supported: Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Bristol, Cardiff, Edinburgh as genuine centres, not satellites
- The rail investment the North was promised and denied — delivered
- Broadcasting and cultural funding decentralised
- New cities located deliberately outside the South East

---

## 3.9 CAPITAL REDISTRIBUTION

Split the capital function — as Germany, Australia, Netherlands, and South Africa already do.

- **Parliamentary and Government Capital: Birmingham or Manchester** — geographically central, economically symbolic, transformative signal that Britain has genuinely changed. Parliament's upcoming renovation is the moment to relocate it.
- **Financial Capital: London** — the City stays. It isn't moving and shouldn't.
- **Judicial Capital: independently determined**

---

## 3.10 WATER INFRASTRUCTURE

Britain loses approximately 3 billion litres of water per day through leaking pipes. This is systemic failure, not maintenance backlog.

- National water infrastructure audit and full upgrade programme
- Water companies under public oversight with binding leakage targets
- New cities built with modern water infrastructure standard
- Drought resilience built into all new infrastructure planning

---

## 3.11 DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE

Broadband is infrastructure. It is as essential as roads and electricity. Treating it as a commercial product produces the same result as treating roads as commercial products — coverage gaps, rural exclusion, and poor quality where margins are thin.

- National gigabit broadband as public infrastructure — universal coverage including rural areas
- Public ownership of the core digital backbone
- Cybersecurity treated as national defence infrastructure

---

## 3.12 LISTED & DERELICT BUILDINGS

**Listed Buildings:** The current system fails in both directions — buildings rotting because listing prevents repair, and buildings protected that have no credible future. Reform:
- Tiered review of listed status — with obligations on owners to maintain
- Repair pathways that work within listing requirements
- Demolition permitted for genuinely unsalvageable structures, with replacement requirements

**Derelict Buildings:** Empty, decaying buildings are a blight and a waste.
- Compulsory purchase at fair value
- Rebuild to modern standards or convert to greenspace
- No long-term dereliction tolerated in populated areas

---

## 3.13 GREENSPACE

More of it. Everywhere.
- Greenspace requirements in all urban planning — not optional, not squeezed out
- Rooftop gardens supported and incentivised
- Parks and open spaces protected from development and properly maintained
- Dark sky protection — light pollution regulated in new developments; affects sleep, wildlife, and mental health
- Rewilding corridors alongside new infrastructure

# PILLAR FOUR: STRONG COMMUNITIES
## Health, Housing, Welfare & Culture

---

## 4.1 THE NHS

Major sustained investment. Not as crisis management but as a strategic national asset.

- Funding secured at a level that matches actual need
- Mental health services given full parity with physical health — same funding priority, same waiting time targets
- Preventative care prioritised — treating problems before they become crises saves money and saves lives
- Social care integrated properly with NHS — the current boundary between them causes enormous harm
- **National Drug Development Programme** — Britain invests in developing medicines, owns the intellectual property, and supplies the NHS at cost. Reduces long-term drug expenditure substantially.
- **Pharmaceutical Price Negotiation Body** — negotiates NHS drug costs with genuine bargaining power. We currently pay far more than comparable countries.

---

## 4.2 HOUSING

**Social Housing:** Large-scale construction programme — not the marginal additions of recent decades but genuine scale.

**Resident Cooperative Housing:** Community-managed housing with clear legal ownership structure, financial safeguards, and conflict resolution mechanisms. People with genuine stake in their homes and communities.

**New Planned Cities:** (See 3.6) — supply solution at genuine scale.

**Planning Reform:** Fast-track approval for housing, energy, and transport infrastructure — one of Britain's biggest bottlenecks is planning delay.

**Agricultural land protection:** Fields are for food, not solar farms or speculative development. Grade 1 and 2 agricultural land protected by default.

---

## 4.3 WELFARE

- Welfare reform — simplify, reduce cliff-edges, treat people with dignity
- **Universal Basic Income: piloted properly** — not vague aspiration but real pilots with rigorous evaluation. Evidence gathered before national rollout decisions made.
- Means-testing reformed where it costs more to administer than it saves
- Transitions into work supported — no sudden loss of support before employment is stable

---

## 4.4 VETERANS

Britain asks people to risk their lives in service. The obligation does not end when they leave.

- Mental health support for veterans — properly funded, specifically designed, not generic referrals
- Employment transition support — real, practical, individually tailored
- Housing priority for veterans in need
- Physical health support — including conditions specific to military service
- No veteran should be homeless. This is a statement of intent backed by policy and funding.

---

## 4.5 CULTURE, ARTS & COMMUNITY

**Arts Funding:** Restored and expanded — theatre, music, dance, visual arts. Particularly for children and young people, who need somewhere to go, something to do, and outlets for creative energy.

**Community Spaces:** Multi-faith halls in each city — shared spaces for reflection, community gathering, and interfaith engagement. Not belonging to any single tradition. Open to all.

**Sauna Culture:** Community sauna facilities alongside swimming pools and leisure centres — social, healthy, accessible to all incomes. A genuine public wellness infrastructure investment.

**Libraries:** Statutory protection, ring-fenced funding, extended hours. (See Pillar Five.)

**Legal Nudity:** Non-sexual public nudity decriminalised with reasonable context protections. Bodily autonomy is a personal liberty. This harms nobody.

---

## 4.6 FOOD & CONSUMER STANDARDS

**Best-Before Date Reform:** The current three-label system (Use By, Best Before, Sell By) confuses consumers and causes enormous unnecessary food waste. Replace with one standardised system, one consistent label, clear public education. Simple, cheap, effective.

**Food Packaging:** Mandatory recyclable or compostable packaging with a clear industry phase-in timeline.

**Lab-grown and sustainable food:** Supported and developed — Britain leads in food technology, reducing pressure on land and supply chains.

**Food security:** Self-sufficiency in staple foods a national strategic goal. British farmers supported through fair subsidies and protection from unfair imports.

**Universal Free School Meals:** (See Pillar Five.)

---

## 4.7 JUSTICE

- Strong enforcement against serious crimes — rape, child abuse, abuse of power
- Enhanced sentencing for those in positions of trust who commit crimes
- Faster courts — justice delayed is justice denied for victims and defendants both
- Victim-focused reform — better support, better evidence handling, better communication
- **Drug Policy:** Legalise and regulate on a tiered harm-based system (see below)

---

## 4.8 DRUG POLICY

Current system: widely criticised, inconsistent, evidence-free.

**Reform:**
- Replace the classification system with an evidence-based harm index: physical harm, addiction potential, social harm
- Tier 1 (low risk — cannabis-type): legal, regulated, taxed
- Tier 2 (moderate risk): controlled access via licensed vendors
- Tier 3 (high risk): available only through medical supervision or addiction treatment
- Decriminalise possession for personal use across all tiers
- Focus enforcement on trafficking, exploitation, and illegal supply
- Tax revenue earmarked for NHS, addiction services, and education
- Portugal's model demonstrates this approach works; Britain can do it better

---

## 4.9 SEX WORK POLICY

- Legalised and regulated — bringing activity into safe, monitored environments
- Worker health and safety standards, legal protection from abuse, right to refuse clients
- Severe penalties for trafficking, coercion, and underage involvement — enforcement focused here, not on workers
- Funded exit pathways: education, employment, housing support for those who wish to leave the industry
- Core principle: protect people, not impose moral judgements

---

## 4.10 NOISE POLLUTION

Seriously underregulated. Serious health consequences. Nobody addresses it.

- National noise standards for residential areas, updated for modern sources
- Enforcement regime with real consequences
- New development design standards — noise impact assessed and mitigated

# PILLAR FIVE: PREPARE THE FUTURE
## Education, Employment & Democratic Literacy

---

## 5.1 EDUCATION — WHAT SCHOOLS SHOULD ACTUALLY TEACH

**Academic Foundation:** Maintained and strengthened.

**Life Skills — Added, Mandatory:**
- Critical thinking, research skills, bias identification, media literacy
- Financial literacy: wages, taxes, pensions, UBI, payslips, contracts
- Civic literacy: how Parliament works, how to vote, what local councils do
- Employment preparation: CVs, cover letters, interviews, workplace rights
- Personal finance: budgeting, renting, utilities, basic cooking, first aid, mental health literacy

**Vocational Pathways:** Equal in standing to academic routes. Not a consolation prize. Apprenticeships properly funded and supervised.

**Free School Meals:** Universal at primary level. Phased expansion to secondary. Holiday hunger programmes funded. Nutritional standards enforced.

**Arts Protected:** Ringfenced in curricula. After-school programmes funded. Youth theatre, choir, music, visual arts — not extras, but essentials.

**Graffiti & Street Art Centres:** Legal walls and dedicated spaces where young people learn spray art and mural design. Evidence shows legal walls reduce tagging substantially. Redirect creative energy rather than criminalise it.

**SEND Reform:** Earlier identification, diversified teaching methods, diversified assessment, improved EHCP process.

---

## 5.2 EMPLOYMENT REFORM

**Advertised Salaries: Mandatory.** All job adverts must state actual salary. "Competitive" and "DOE" are banned. The salary question in interviews is redundant because the answer is already public.

**Banned Interview Questions:**
- Hypothetical personality questions
- Personal life, family, relationship status
- Salary history
- Any question irrelevant to job performance

**Alternative Assessment Formats:** Work trials, portfolio reviews, task assessments — supported and incentivised. Public sector leads.

**Neurodivergent Adaptations:** Reasonable adjustments in hiring strengthened and enforced. Questions provided in advance as standard option.

**Entry-Level Reform:** Entry-level roles must be genuinely entry-level. No experience required for starter positions.

**4-Day Working Week:** Phased transition where viable. Public sector pilots first. Evidence-based rollout.

**Equal Pay Regardless of Age:** The work is worth the wage. Age-based pay tiers abolished.

---

## 5.3 DEMOCRATIC LITERACY

**Transparent Tax Spending:** (See Pillar One — 1.6)

**Civic Education:** How Parliament works, how to vote, how councils function, how to contact representatives, how public services work — mandatory in schools, supported through libraries.

**Critical Thinking:** Media literacy, online literacy, political literacy — taught in schools and supported through public libraries and community education.

**Libraries:** Statutory protection. Ring-fenced funding. Extended hours. Digital and physical services maintained.

# PILLAR SIX: BRITAIN IN THE WORLD
## Defence, Trade & Global Strategy

---

## 6.1 DEFENCE

Strong, modern, capable military — not for aggression but for credible deterrence.

- Cyber defence investment: treated as core national defence infrastructure
- Domestic defence industry capability — not dependent on foreign supply for critical military equipment
- Veterans properly supported (see 4.4)
- Crisis planning: war, pandemic, economic crash — contingency capacity maintained

---

## 6.2 IMMIGRATION

- Strong border control and enforcement
- Skilled migration pathways — clear, fair, efficient
- Integration requirements: language, employment, civic participation
- Equal legal standards for all — same rules, same responsibilities, same consequences
- Voting rights after permanent residency and 5 years of residence

---

## 6.3 GLOBAL STRATEGY

Replace the outdated foreign aid model with:

- **Global Partnership Network** — accountable, results-based international investment in trade, science, and infrastructure
- Britain as a hub of influence, not control — partnerships of equals, not post-imperial paternalism
- UK leadership in food technology, pharmaceutical development, clean energy, and AI — genuine global contribution through excellence

# SOCIAL POLICY

## Equality & Identity

- Full legal recognition of non-binary identities
- Gender transition recognition — streamlined, respectful legal processes
- Protection from discrimination under law

## Relationships

The state recognises all committed relationships between consenting adults, provided they meet standards of consent, transparency, and legal accountability:

- Civil Marriage (2+ consenting adults, equal regardless of gender)
- Civil Partnership
- Common-Law Partnership (recognised after defined cohabitation period)
- Registered Multi-Partner Unions (with enhanced legal safeguards, independent legal advice, full financial disclosure, cooling-off periods)
- Religious or Cultural Unions (recognised only when registered with the state)

## Sign Language

- BSL supported and promoted nationally
- Required in key professions: healthcare, education, emergency services
- Sign language as a curriculum option in all schools

## Separation of Church and State

- Legal recognition of relationships determined solely by civil law
- Religious institutions may perform ceremonies but hold no independent legal authority
