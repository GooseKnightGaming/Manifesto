## 1.2 ELECTORAL REFORM

**Ranked Weighted Voting replaces First Past the Post.**

Voters rank candidates in preference order. Points awarded by ranking. Winners must command broad support — not just an intense minority.

Results:
- Every vote counts, not just those in marginal seats
- Extreme polarisation reduced — candidates need appeal beyond their core
- Tactical voting eliminated — vote for who you actually want
- Cooperation incentivised — parties must speak to a wide electorate

**Compulsory Participation — The Australian Model.**

Every eligible citizen participates. Spoiled ballots and None of the Above are fully valid. Conscientious non-participation is replaced by formal registration of dissatisfaction.

*On the civil liberties objection:* We are not compelling a political choice. We are compelling participation in the process. The NOTA option means a citizen who rejects all candidates can register that rejection formally — counted, published, and taken seriously as a democratic signal. Invisible abstention is replaced by visible protest.

*On the legal question:* A Human Rights Act compatibility assessment will be published before legislation is introduced. If compulsory participation is found incompatible with the HRA, we will introduce maximum-incentive voluntary participation — strong positive incentives for voting — as an alternative. The goal is full democratic engagement. The mechanism adapts to the legal framework.

**NOTA — What Happens If NOTA Wins:**

A fresh election is held within 60 days. Original candidates are ineligible to stand. The constituency is temporarily served by a caretaker drawn from the National Oversight Council. This cannot be gamed repeatedly — the costs and logistics of multiple by-elections deter organised spoiling, and compulsory participation means NOTA must genuinely reflect majority sentiment to win.

**Coalition Red Lines — Stated Before Any Election:**

Britain Forward will not enter coalition with any party that:
- Opposes ranked weighted voting
- Seeks to reverse public ownership of core infrastructure
- Abandons the ministerial competence requirement

Everything else is negotiable. These three are not. Voters know this in advance.

## 1.3 CONSTITUTIONAL FRAMEWORK

**The Monarchy — Constitutional Custodian of Last Resort**

The Monarch is not the sovereign. Sovereignty resides in the British people, expressed through Parliament and elections.

The Monarch is a *constitutional custodian* — a non-political safeguard ensuring no single actor can capture the entire constitutional apparatus. In genuine constitutional crisis only, the Monarch acts to restore the conditions under which popular sovereignty can be exercised. They reign. They do not govern.

Largely ceremonial. Largely silent. Present and real when needed.

**The House of Lords — Reform by Public Decision**

The Lords in its current form is indefensible — unelected, unaccountable, filled with political appointees and inherited privilege.

Its *function* — scrutiny, revision, long-term perspective — is valuable and must be preserved.

Britain Forward will not impose a constitutional settlement. We will:
- Publish three reform options with full analysis
- Consult the public
- Hold a binding referendum on which model Britain chooses

Options include:
- Full replacement with an elected Senate — fixed terms, regional representation
- Hybrid chamber — partially elected, partially independently expert-appointed
- Full abolition — functions transferred to a strengthened National Oversight Council

The public decides. Not us.

**National Oversight Council**

Established as a scrutiny body alongside Lords reform:
- Composition: elected members plus independently appointed experts (judiciary, science, ethics, civic society) — no government appointments, ever
- Selection: fully independent appointment process, publicly transparent
- Powers: delay and refer legislation for review — not permanently block
- Integrated with judicial review

**Parliament — London Now, Direction Set**

Parliament remains in London this Parliament.

- Government departments: phased relocation to regional cities begins immediately
- Regional parliamentary offices opened in major cities — genuine presence
- An independent commission assesses and plans Parliament's future location — reports within five years
- If relocation is recommended: public referendum decides

The direction is clear. The process is honest.

## 1.4 POLITICAL ACCOUNTABILITY

**Conflict of Interest — The Full Standard**

The principle: MPs do not personally benefit from policies they shape.

The *distinction that matters*: sector expertise is *encouraged* — a doctor becoming Health Secretary should not be disqualified. Personal financial benefit from specific decisions is *prohibited.*

**The test:** Did this company or individual materially benefit from a specific decision this MP made or influenced?

*If yes:*
- MPs must not have had direct involvement with any company passing that test in the previous **5 years** before taking office
- MPs must not be involved with any such company **while serving**
- MPs must not join any such company for **10 years after** leaving office

*On family members:* Where demonstrable financial benefit flows to an MP *through* a family connection — where the MP materially gains via a spouse or family member's position — the same standard applies. We acknowledge this is legally complex. The test is flowing benefit to the MP, not mere family proximity.

**MP Pay — Public Service Standard**

Being an MP is civic duty, not a career enrichment opportunity.

- MPs are paid the **national median wage** with a cost-of-living adjustment for London — they experience economic reality rather than a political premium
- Genuine work expenses covered: accommodation for MPs representing constituencies requiring regular London travel — a room or flat, not a second property purchase
- **Zero outside income** while serving — full attention to constituents
- All expenses: fully transparent, publicly searchable, audited quarterly
- Personal and family expenses: never eligible
- The standard: "Would a reasonable constituent accept this as a legitimate work cost?" If not, it isn't.
- Pay rises: subject to the same process as public sector pay — not self-determined

## 2.2 NATIONALISATION — THE THREE-TIER MODEL

Public ownership of core national infrastructure. Not the 1970s. The Norwegian, French, and German model — brought to Britain.

**Why:** Water, energy networks, and oil are natural monopolies or strategic national resources. Competition does not work in pipes and wires. Profit extraction from essential services transfers wealth from citizens to shareholders — often foreign ones. Every comparable successful economy maintains public ownership or strong public control of core infrastructure. Britain is the outlier.

**On international trade law:** Public ownership of infrastructure is explicitly permitted under WTO rules and all trade agreements Britain has signed. Every WTO member has publicly owned infrastructure. Legal opinions from leading international trade counsel will be published before any nationalisation legislation is introduced.

**Tier One — Infrastructure Layer: 100% Publicly Owned**

The physical networks — pipes, wires, cables, rails — owned outright by the British public, managed by public bodies.

*British Water. British Grid. British Rail Infrastructure.*

**Direct accountability:** When water is contaminated, British Water is accountable. Not a private company, then an internal review, then an independent review, then a regulator, then a tribunal. One body. Direct accountability. Real consequences.

**Acquisition mechanism:**
- Phased public bond issuance — spread over years, financed by the asset's own revenues
- Regulatory Asset Base model — acquisition at independently assessed physical asset value, not inflated market valuation including profit expectations
- Several water companies are currently in financial distress — near-insolvent companies can be acquired through administration at a fraction of market value. The private sector's failures are our opportunity.
- The asset pays for the acquisition over time through its own revenues

**Operational transition:**
- Existing regulators (Ofwat, Ofgem) immediately repurposed as transitional public bodies
- Existing operational management retained during transition — we are changing ownership, not firing everyone who knows how a water treatment plant works
- Service continuity a legal requirement throughout — written into enabling legislation
- Full operational transition: 5-7 years per sector, beginning with water
- Legislation in year one. Operations transferred progressively. Nothing switched off overnight.

**Tier Two — Operating Layer: Majority Public, Citizen Shares**

Companies operating *through* the public infrastructure:
- Government holds majority ownership (51-75%)
- Remaining shares available to British citizens directly — not institutional investors, not foreign funds
- Citizens receive annual dividends — a genuine stake in their own country's assets
- Operating companies retain some market discipline while profit primarily serves the public
- Citizens who hold shares may vote at shareholder meetings on operational matters, within the governance framework

**Tier Three — Competitive Layer: Private and Free**

Services and innovations built *on top of* the public infrastructure compete freely. The network is public. What runs on it competes. Innovation is encouraged, protected, and rewarded at this level.

*The analogy: the internet's publicly-funded backbone, with private innovation on top. Public infrastructure enabling private excellence.*

## 2.3 UNIVERSAL BASIC INCOME — THE FULL FRAMEWORK

UBI is not a radical experiment. It is a rational simplification of a benefits system that currently costs billions to administer and still fails millions of people.

**The Rate:** Approximately £100 per week is the aspirational target rate. Pilots will begin at a pilotable rate. National rollout rate is confirmed by evidence from pilots and independent economic modelling — not committed to before that evidence exists.

**Net cost after recoupture and system replacement:**
- Higher earners have UBI taxed back through marginal income tax rates
- The complex, expensive means-tested benefits system is substantially replaced
- Net additional cost requires independent economic modelling — this will be commissioned and published before national rollout
- Gross figure (£100/week × all recipients) is not the meaningful number; net cost after tax recoupture and system savings is

**Who receives UBI — the contribution threshold model:**

UBI is not a free gift. It is a floor — earned through participation in and contribution to British society.

| Group | UBI | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| British citizens | ✅ Full | From birth, effectively |
| Settled residents (5+ years) | ✅ Full | After contribution threshold met |
| High-skilled visa holders | ✅ After 2 years | Shorter threshold recognising economic contribution |
| General visa holders | ✅ After 5 years | Standard contribution threshold |
| Asylum seekers/refugees | Needs-based support | Separate framework, outside UBI system |
| Pensioners | ✅ Full UBI | Plus earned pension |
| Working people | ✅ Full UBI | Plus earned wage |
| Job-seeking unemployed | ✅ Full UBI | Plus active employment support |
| Non-seeking, non-disabled unemployed | ✅ Full UBI | This is their income. It is sufficient for a quiet life. |
| Disabled people | ✅ Full UBI | Plus separate needs-based disability support |
| Students | ✅ Full UBI | Replaces most maintenance support |
| People under criminal sentence | ❌ Suspended | Resumes on day of release — not permanently lost |
| Foreign nationals without qualifying residency | ❌ Not eligible | — |

**On the "laziness" objection:**

Evidence from UBI pilots in Finland, Kenya, Canada, and Alaska consistently shows UBI *increases* workforce participation. People in jobs they chose, that they can afford to take, stay longer and perform better. People who don't need to take any available job just to survive find jobs that suit them — and those matches last.

Some people will live on UBI alone and not work. They will have a safe, austere, very quiet life without luxuries. This is their choice. It costs the state less than the current system of means-testing, sanctions, appeals, and tribunals around the same people.

**On the "equal responsibility" principle:**

Equal responsibility applies to people in equivalent circumstances. A British citizen with decades of contribution and a recent arrival are not in equivalent circumstances. The UBI qualifying threshold is like earning pension rights — you contribute first, you receive after. Once qualified, the rules are identical for everyone regardless of origin.

**On criminal sentence suspension:**

UBI is suspended *during sentence*, not permanently. Upon release, UBI resumes on day one. The punishment is losing the UBI that would have accrued during the sentence — not being left destitute on exit. Released prisoners receive UBI immediately upon release, plus an active resettlement support package. Destitution on release increases reoffending. We are not interested in increasing reoffending.

**Implementation:** This Parliament establishes properly evaluated pilots. The following Parliament makes the national rollout decision based on pilot evidence. This is not delay — it is responsible policy development for a system that will affect every person in Britain.

## 2.4 THE "BENEFIT FROM BRITAIN" PRINCIPLE

Britain's resources — energy, water, oil, land — primarily benefit Britain.

This is not economic nationalism. It is basic logic applied to a country that has spent decades selling its national assets to foreign shareholders and then buying back the output at market prices.

The distinction: we are talking about who *profits* from what Britain *produces* — currently often foreign pension funds, hedge funds, and sovereign wealth funds of other countries. We are not talking about who *lives* here or who *belongs* here.

When British oil is extracted, the profit funds British public services — not Norwegian pension funds. When British water flows through British pipes, the returns go to British citizens — not private equity firms registered offshore.

This is not Britain First. It is Britain's resources for Britain's people.

## 3.1 ROADS — REBUILT TO LAST

British roads are resurfaced constantly because cheap materials degrade quickly. We pay again and again for the same roads.

- Long-life composite surfaces — built to last decades, not years
- Whole-life costing in all road contracts — the cheapest road upfront is never the cheapest road overall
- Road-embedded EV charging — inductive coils during rebuild; vehicles charge while moving; solves the commercial payload problem (a laden electric van currently manages approximately 50 miles; continuous road charging removes that constraint entirely and makes commercial EVs viable)
- Road hierarchies redesigned for modern freight, cycling, and future autonomous vehicles
- Green corridors alongside all new roads — wildlife routes integrated into engineering from the start
- Usage-based road pricing — replaces fuel duty as EVs grow; fair, transparent, hypothecated to roads and transport

## 3.2 PUBLIC DIGNITY INFRASTRUCTURE

*The basics. Working. For everyone.*
*Not charity. Infrastructure.*

**Public Toilets**
- Statutory minimum provision — councils cannot cut below the floor
- Ring-fenced central government funding
- All A-road laybys and rest stops: free facilities, maintained, lit, safe
- HGV rest areas upgraded substantially — showers, proper facilities, safe lighting. Truckers are legally required to take rest breaks. The infrastructure to do so must exist.
- Petrol stations on public roads: free toilet access required for any public contract or licence
- Changing Places facilities (for people requiring hoists, space, and full accessibility) required in all new major public buildings

**Public Showers**

- Serves homeless people, outdoor workers, cyclists, and anyone who needs them

**Public Kitchens**
- Accessible, equipped cooking spaces at community centres, libraries, and dedicated facilities

- Liability and hygiene: operated to full FSA commercial kitchen standards; same framework as any public leisure facility
- Not a soup kitchen. A kitchen. Open to everyone.

**Public Orchards & Community Growing**

- Community growing spaces alongside — allotments, raised beds, shared gardens
- Connected to school growing programmes and culinary education

## 3.3 ENERGY — BRITAIN POWERS BRITAIN

**Goal: Britain does not depend on foreign energy for essentials.**

**Renewables:**
- Solar: rooftops, not fields. Fields grow food. Roofs generate power. Mandatory on all new residential and commercial builds. Retrofit programme — social housing and low-income households first.
- Wind: onshore and offshore expansion
- Tidal and wave: Britain has some of the world's best tidal resources and is barely exploiting them. This is a strategic opportunity being ignored. It will not be ignored.
- Hydro: Scotland's river network supports community micro-hydro schemes across dozens of sites — each local scheme powering a local town frees national grid capacity. Aggregate effect is substantial.
- Geothermal: Cornwall's deep geothermal project as the expansion template for suitable geology across Britain

**Nuclear:** Stable baseload alongside variable renewables. Investment in next-generation reactor designs.

**Oil and gas:** British production maintained during energy transition — not abandoned prematurely. British production for British need first. Surplus sold internationally. Revenue to Sovereign Wealth Fund.

**Smart grid:** Upgraded for distributed rooftop generation. Excess household power bought back at fair rates — energy bills become energy income for many households.

**District heating:** Shared heat networks in new cities and major urban areas — efficient, lower carbon than individual boiler systems, cheaper for residents.

## 3.4 HIGH-SPEED RAIL — ENGINEERING, NOT POLITICS

Japan builds high-speed rail through earthquake zones on mountainous islands. On time. On budget. Because they treat it as an engineering project, not a political one.

Britain is flat, geologically stable, and comparatively small. The argument against high-speed rail in Britain is not engineering. It is political will and fiscal priority.

HS2 failed because it was mismanaged, overscoped, and subjected to political interference at every stage. That is a lesson in how not to do it. It is not an argument against doing it.

**Britain Forward commits:**
- A properly scoped, independent, engineering-led high-speed rail programme
- Engineering decisions made by engineers — an independent delivery body insulated from political interference
- Transparent budget with genuine contingency, published before a shovel breaks ground
- Routes prioritised by economic benefit — not political geography
- This is a multi-Parliament direction, not a single-Parliament promise. The direction will not change.

**Best-Before Date Reform:** Three labels (Use By, Best Before, Sell By) cause enormous unnecessary food waste through consumer confusion. One standardised label. One consistent system. Clear public education. Simple, cheap, effective.

**Food Packaging:** Mandatory recyclable or compostable packaging — clear industry phase-in timeline.

**Lab-grown and sustainable food:** Supported and developed — Britain leads in food technology.

**Food Security:** Self-sufficiency in staple foods as a strategic national goal. British farmers supported through fair subsidy and protection from unfair imports.

## 4.7 POLICIES UNDER ACTIVE EVIDENCE REVIEW

Britain Forward believes in personal liberty and evidence-based regulation. The following will be legislated when the evidence and legal frameworks are sufficiently robust to do it safely and effectively.

We state this openly. We could have hidden these policies. We have not.

**Drug Policy Reform**

The current classification system is widely criticised by experts as inconsistent and ineffective. International evidence — from Portugal's decriminalisation, Canada's cannabis regulation, the Netherlands' coffee shop system — demonstrates that treating drug use as a health issue rather than a criminal one reduces harm, enables victim identification and protection, and allows enforcement to focus on trafficking and exploitation rather than users.

*Specific criteria for action:* When evidence demonstrates that a regulated harm-based model produces lower overall harm than the current criminalised model in a British cultural and legal context, and when a legal and operational framework for implementation has been fully developed, this policy will be brought forward within one Parliamentary session.

The evidence review is underway. The criteria are public. The timeline follows the evidence.

**Sex Work Policy**

Legal, regulated sex work enables worker health and safety protections, identification and protection of trafficking victims (far easier in a regulated environment than a criminalised one), and enforcement focused on exploitation rather than workers.

*Criteria:* When evidence from New Zealand, Germany, and other regulatory models demonstrates which framework best protects workers and reduces exploitation in a British context, and when a full operational and legal framework has been developed, this policy will be brought forward.

**Relationship Law Reform**

Legal recognition of diverse adult relationship structures — with full safeguards, independent legal advice requirements, and clear frameworks for finances, parental responsibility, and dissolution — is under active legal review.

**Personal Liberty and Decriminalisation**

Britain Forward believes that what consenting adults do in ways that harm nobody else is not the state's business. The practical and legal frameworks for specific decriminalisations are under review.

*These are not hidden policies. The direction is stated plainly. The timing is determined by evidence and legal readiness — not by electoral calculation and not by infinite deferral.*

## 5.2 EMPLOYMENT REFORM

**Advertised Salaries: Mandatory Without Exception.**
All job advertisements state actual salary or salary range. "Competitive" and "DOE" are banned. The salary question in interviews is abolished — the answer is already public. This eliminates a negotiation that disproportionately disadvantages women, neurodivergent people, and those from lower-income backgrounds.

**Banned Interview Questions:**
- Hypothetical personality questions with no job-performance relationship ("if you were a fish...")
- Questions about personal life, family plans, or relationship status
- Salary history questions
- Any question whose answer could be used for unlawful discrimination

**Alternative Assessment Formats:** Work trials, portfolio reviews, task assessments, and written application stages — actively encouraged and publicly supported. Public sector leads by example, providing the model.

**Neurodivergent Adaptations:** Interview questions provided in advance as a standard available option — helps neurodivergent candidates and produces better-considered answers from everyone. Reasonable adjustments in hiring: strengthened enforcement.

**Entry-Level Standards:** Entry-level roles must be genuinely entry-level — experience requirements for genuinely starter positions are banned.

**Workplace Rights:**
- 4-day working week: phased where viable, public sector pilots first, evidence-based rollout
- Equal pay regardless of age — the work is worth the wage, regardless of who does it
- Zero-hours contract reform: guaranteed minimum hours for anyone employed regularly
- Right to disconnect: employers cannot routinely require availability outside contracted hours
- Corporate pay ratio limits

# PILLAR SIX: BRITAIN IN THE WORLD
## Defence, Trade & Global Strategy

---

## 6.1 DEFENCE — STRONG, SOVEREIGN, NOT FOR HIRE

Britain will be strong. Britain will be prepared. Britain will not be a tool of other nations' agendas.

**Britain WILL:**
- Honour Article 5 of the NATO treaty — full stop. If a NATO ally faces genuine military aggression, Britain responds. This is a treaty obligation Britain has made and will keep.
- Maintain strong, modern, capable military capability — deterrence and genuine defence
- Invest in cyber defence as core national infrastructure
- Support domestic defence industry — not dependent on foreign supply for critical capability
- Support veterans properly (see Pillar Four)

**Britain WILL NOT:**
- Participate in offensive military operations outside Article 5 contexts without full parliamentary debate, a clear legal basis, and genuine British security interest
- Be pressured into military commitments that serve other nations' agendas rather than Britain's genuine security
- Commit troops or weapons without democratic mandate and public accountability

**The distinction:** Defending allies against genuine aggression — always, as treaty requires. Military adventures in other nations' conflicts on behalf of allied partners — only with full parliamentary mandate and genuine British security justification. The difference between defence and adventure is real, important, and will be maintained.

---

## 6.2 IMMIGRATION

- Strong border control — enforced, fair, consistent
- Skilled migration pathways — clear, efficient, merit-based; Britain remains open to the talent it needs
- Integration requirements: language, civic participation
- Equal legal standards — same rules, same responsibilities, same consequences for everyone
- UBI and voting rights after permanent residency and five years of residence — the contribution threshold
- High-skilled visa holders: shorter UBI qualifying period (two years) in recognition of economic contribution
- Foreign nationals who commit serious crimes: deported upon completion of sentence
- Asylum seekers: processed fairly, promptly, and humanely — needs-based support outside the UBI framework during processing

---

## 6.3 GLOBAL STRATEGY

**The EU Question:** Britain Forward does not make EU membership a priority of this platform in either direction. We will maintain constructive, productive relationships with European partners — on science, research, education, and trade where agreements serve Britain. We will not re-litigate Brexit as an end in itself. If the evidence ever supports a formal change in Britain's relationship with the EU, it will be put to the British public in a referendum. It is not our decision to make unilaterally.

**Global Partnership:** Replace outdated foreign aid with accountable, results-based international investment — trade, science, infrastructure. Britain as a hub of excellence: medicine, food technology, clean energy, engineering, AI. Partnerships of equals. Not post-imperial paternalism.

Britain sustains itself first. Britain trades its strengths globally. Britain imports luxuries and specialisms freely. Other countries are needed for what Britain doesn't produce — not for what Britain can produce itself.
